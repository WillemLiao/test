Wonder段十三 · Summer 的视角 · 真正的朋友

R. J. Palacio
第三部分从 Summer 的视角讲述。她只是出于同情坐在 Auggie 旁边,却成了他最好的朋友。"瘟疫"游戏、Savanna 的派对、关于轮回的对话——Summer 用她的善良和勇气,向所有人证明了什么是真正的友谊。
1
怪小孩
Weird Kids · "He's just a kid"
动机选择

"Because he's a nice kid!" I always answer. "And don't call him that."

"You're a saint, Summer," Ximena Chin said. "I couldn't do what you're doing." "It's not a big deal."

"Did Mr. Tushman ask you to be friends with him?" Charlotte asked. "No. I'm friends with him because I want to be."

Who knew that my sitting with August would be such a big deal? People acted like it was the strangest thing. I sat with him that first day because I felt sorry for him. That's all. Julian had nicknamed him the Zombie Kid. "Did you see the Zombie Kid yet?"

He's just a kid. The weirdest-looking kid I've ever seen, yes. But just a kid.

✦ 就只是一个孩子

Summer 的答案简单到让所有人惊讶——没有什么高尚的理由,就是因为他是个人,一个孩子。

2
瘟疫
The Plague · "No one touches August"
残忍游戏

I do admit August's face takes some getting used to. He's not the neatest eater. But he's fun. One of the things I'm not loving about this year is how kids act too grown-up. August likes to play Four Square at recess. I love that.

That's how I found out about the Plague. Apparently it's a "game" — anyone who accidentally touches August has thirty seconds to wash their hands before they catch it. Maya told me the reason she won't play Four Square is that she doesn't want to catch the Plague. I told her it was dumb. She agreed, but she still wouldn't touch a ball that August just touched.

✦ 看不见的瘟疫

孩子间的"游戏"往往是最残忍的。没有人真的相信瘟疫存在,但每个人都配合演出。

3
万圣节派对
The Halloween Party · "You could be popular"
选择原则

I got invited to Savanna's Halloween party — the most popular girl in school. No costumes allowed. When I got there, Savanna asked: "Where's your boyfriend, Summer?" She was talking about August.

"You would be a lot more popular if you didn't hang out with him so much. Julian likes you. He wants to ask you out." "So you have to choose who you want to hang out with."

I looked over at Julian. Then I went to the bathroom, called my mom to pick me up, and snuck out. I hung out in the bathroom until Mom called, got my jacket, and went outside. It was only nine-thirty. The Halloween Parade was in full swing. Everyone was in costume. But not one unicorn.

✦ 一只独角兽都没有

Summer 选择了离开派对,而不是背叛 Auggie。那个关于独角兽的结尾——她宁可做一只没人看到的独角兽,也不做受欢迎的伪君子。

4
十一月
November · "You don't have to be friends with me"
信任拉勾

August was acting so weird at lunch after Halloween. Barely said a word. Wouldn't look me in the eye.

"You know, Summer," he said. "You don't have to do this. You don't have to be friends with me. I know Mr. Tushman talked to you."

I swore on my life he didn't. I shook off my UGGs so he could see my toes weren't crossed. "You're wearing tights," he said accusingly. "You can see my toes are flat!" I yelled. He finally believed me. Then he told me about Halloween — what Jack said. "Promise you won't tell anyone. Promise you won't ever be mean like that to me again?" We pinky swore.

✦ 光脚的证明

Summer 脱鞋证明自己不是被安排的——这个画面比任何誓言都更有力。信任的建立有时候只需要一个真诚的举动。

5
R 级警告
Warning: This Kid Is Rated R · Reincarnation
生死轮回

August came over to my house for the first time. My mom was shocked when she saw his face — I hadn't prepared her enough. "Stop making that weirded-out face!" I whispered.

Auggie saw a picture of my dad. "Is that your dad?" "He was a platoon sergeant. He died a few years ago."

We talked about what happens when people die. "I think when people die, their souls go to heaven for a while, then get born again as brand-new babies. Their souls get a chance to have a do-over."

He thought about it. "That means in my next life I won't be stuck with this face." He pointed to his face and batted his eyes. "I might even be handsome! That would be so awesome, wouldn't it?"

He told me about his condition: mandibulofacial dysostosis. "I don't want to brag, but I'm considered something of a medical wonder." "You can laugh," he said. "I am cool beans."

✦ 下一次机会

Auggie 用轮回的观念来安慰自己——下次投胎他就好看了。这份幽默里藏着多少心酸,只有 Summer 懂得。

6
埃及墓室
The Egyptian Tomb · "Bleeding Scream"
线索耳语

Over the next month, August and I hung out a lot. On Egyptian Museum Day, the gym was turned into a giant museum. August made his step pyramid out of sugar cubes, spray-painted with fake-sand paint. It looked awesome.

August and I dressed as mummies — our faces covered except for eye holes and a mouth hole. We took our moms on flashlight tours through the dark gym. It was so much fun, hearing all these whispers in the dark, seeing all the lights zigzagging around the dark room.

At the water fountain, Jack stopped me: "Do you know why August is mad at me?" I nodded. "Can you tell me?" "No. I told him I wouldn't tell you." He seemed so bummed. I whispered in his ear: "Bleeding Scream." And walked away.

✦ 血淋淋的尖叫

Summer 打破了承诺——只说了两个词。"Bleeding Scream" 是 Jack 认出 Auggie 的那天,是他自己说出了那些话的那天。他需要自己去面对。
