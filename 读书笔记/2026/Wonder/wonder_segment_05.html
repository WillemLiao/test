Wonder段五 · 开学第一天 · 课堂与午餐

R. J. Palacio
自我介绍、Darth Sidious 的暗箭、Mr. Browne 的格言"Choose Kind"，以及独自一人坐在空荡荡的餐桌前——开学第一天充满了各种第一次。
1
自我介绍一圈
Around the Room · "Why do you have that tiny braid?"
课堂初印象

Ms. Petosa told us a little about who she was — left Wall Street to pursue her dream of teaching. Julian raised his hand to compliment her. "That's cool about how you're pursuing your dream." He smiled proudly.

She asked us to introduce ourselves — two things we wanted people to know. Julian went first: "I just got Battleground Mystic for my Wii and it's totally awesome. And we got a Ping-Pong table this summer."

Charlotte went next: "I have two sisters, and we just got a new puppy named Suki in July."

Then the teacher called my name. "My name is August. I, um … have a sister named Via and a dog named Daisy. And, um … that's it."

"Wonderful," said Ms. Petosa. "Anyone have questions for August?"

No one said anything.

✦ 无言的沉默

Julian 热情地赞美老师，但到了 Auggie 这里，没有任何人提问。沉默本身已经是一种回答。

2
羔羊入屠场
Lamb to the Slaughter · Darth Sidious
暗箭伤害

"Like a lamb to the slaughter": Something that you say about someone who goes somewhere calmly, not knowing that something unpleasant is going to happen to them. I Googled it last night.

Ms. Petosa called my name. I mumbled my introduction. Then Julian raised his hand: "Why do you have that tiny braid in the back of your hair? Is that like a Padawan thing?"

"Yeah."

He asked who my favorite Star Wars character was. I said Jango Fett. "What about Darth Sidious? Do you like him?"

Maybe no one got the Darth Sidious thing, and maybe Julian didn't mean anything at all. But in Star Wars Episode III, Darth Sidious's face gets burned by Sith lightning and becomes totally deformed. His skin gets all shriveled up and his whole face just kind of melts.

I peeked at Julian and he was looking at me. Yeah, he knew what he was saying.

✦ 暗语

Julian 没有直接说什么——他用《星球大战》的暗语来伤害 Auggie。这种"不是故意的故意"比直接的侮辱更残忍。

3
选择善良
Choose Kind · Mr. Browne's Precept
格言希望

Next class was English with Mr. Browne — a really tall man with a yellow beard. He wrote on the board in big block letters: P-R-E-C-E-P-T!

"A precept is anything that helps guide us when making decisions about really important things."

He asked us what the most important thing was. Kids called out: Rules. Schoolwork. Family. The environment. Sharks. "But no one's named the most important thing of all," he said. "Who we are! What kind of person am I? Isn't that the most important question we should be asking ourselves all the time?"

He told us about the plaque next to the school door: "Know Thyself." Then he wrote in huge letters across the board:

Mr. Browne's September Precept: WHEN GIVEN THE CHOICE BETWEEN BEING RIGHT OR BEING KIND, CHOOSE KIND.

As I wrote it down, I suddenly realized that I was going to like school. No matter what.

✦ Choose Kind

这句话是全书的灵魂。Mr. Browne 用一个简单的格言，给了 Auggie 在黑暗中坚持的理由。

4
午餐
Lunch · "I'm the old moldy cheese"
孤独午餐

Via had warned me about lunch in middle school. All the kids poured into the cafeteria at the same time, talking loudly, saving seats for their friends. I tried to sit at one table, but the kid in the next chair said, "Oh, sorry, but somebody else is sitting here."

I moved to an empty table. Mom had packed me a cheese sandwich, graham crackers, and a juice box.

I could tell I was being stared at without even looking up. I knew that people were nudging each other, watching me out of the corners of their eyes. There was one table of girls whispering behind their hands.

I hate the way I eat. I had surgeries to fix my cleft palate, but I still have a hole in the roof of my mouth. I have to chew food in the front of my mouth. Once, at a birthday party, a kid told the mom he didn't want to sit next to me because I was too messy with all the food crumbs shooting out of my mouth. When I got home, I went to the bathroom mirror and started eating a saltine cracker to see what I looked like. The kid was right. I eat like a tortoise. Like some prehistoric swamp thing.

✦ 独自一人

餐桌上的"有人坐了"是委婉的拒绝。Auggie 对自己吃相的认知，揭示了他一路走来经历了多少次这样的伤害。
