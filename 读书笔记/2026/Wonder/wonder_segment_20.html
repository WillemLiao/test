Wonder段二十 · Miranda 的视角 · 谎言的重量

R. J. Palacio
粉色头发的 Miranda 从未真正消失——她在夏令营里编造了一个关于"畸形弟弟"的谎言,却意外成了最受欢迎的女孩。她换了朋友、染了头发、疏远了 Via。但她打电话给 Auggie 叫他"Major Tom"时声音依然颤抖。首演之夜,她看到台下坐着 Pullman 一家,脱下戏服说"我演不了"。她把聚光灯让给了她最想念的朋友——那个她确实"有资格"当作弟弟的男孩。
1
夏令营的谎言
Camp Lies · "I've kind of earned the right to think of him as my brother."
谎言孤独

Miranda's parents divorced the summer before ninth grade. Her father was already with someone else. Her mother — distant, remote — had a happy face for the world but not much left for her. "I don't know much about what she was like when she was my age. Don't know much about the things she liked or didn't like."

Camp was awful. She didn't know anyone. So she started a little make-believe game: her parents were in Europe; she lived in a huge townhouse; she had a dog named Daisy. Then one day she blurted out that she had a little brother who was deformed. The reaction was dramatic. She regretted it instantly — felt like such a fake. But part of her felt entitled: "I've known Auggie since I was six years old. I've watched him grow up. I've played with him. I've watched all six episodes of Star Wars for his sake. I'm the one that gave him the astronaut helmet he wouldn't take off for two years. I mean, I've kind of earned the right to think of him as my brother."

The lies did wonders for her popularity. For the first time in her life, she was the girl everybody wanted to hang out with. They changed her hair, her makeup, her clothes. She smoked. She snuck through the woods to the boys' camp. When she got home, she called Ella — not Via. Ella was easier. She never asked serious questions. "She wasn't serious like Via. She was fun."

✦ 谎言的资格

Miranda 说"我有资格把他当作弟弟"——这句是全书的钥匙之一。她比任何人都更早进入这个家庭的故事:她给了 Auggie 头盔,她陪他看了六部星战,她是唯一一个在外面能叫他"Major Tom"的人。谎言是错的,但谎言的源头是一种真实的爱。

2
新学校
School · "She hadn't changed at all: we had."
疏远象人

Miranda hardly saw Via at school. When she did, it was awkward. She and Ella badmouthed her: "She's such a prude, she's so this, she's so that." They knew they were being mean, but it was easier to ice her out if they pretended she'd done something. "The truth is she hadn't changed at all: we had. We'd become these other people, and she was still the person she'd always been. That annoyed me so much and I didn't know why."

Halfway through the year, Miranda noticed Justin — the skinny dude with thick glasses and a violin. When she saw him with his arm around Via, she mocked it to Ella, but privately she knew Via was prettier than both of them. Miranda had a boyfriend too: Zack, a varsity jock. "Careful you don't turn into a drama geek."

She wasn't planning on taking theater. Then she saw Via's name on the sign-up sheet and wrote hers down. One day Davenport asked her to photocopy the play he was planning: The Elephant Man. Skimming the pages, she realized what it was about — a terribly deformed man. "We can't do this play, Mr. D. My little brother has a birth defect and has a deformed face and this play would hit too close to home." He ended up switching to Our Town. Miranda went for the role of Emily because she knew Via would, too. She never expected to beat her.

✦ 她没变,是我们变了

Miranda 对 Via 的评价也许是全书最诚实的自我剖析之一——那个还保持着自我的人反而让改变的人感到恼火。而《象人》事件揭示了一个隐藏的链条:正是 Miranda 的干预,让这个故事从关于畸形转向了关于平凡的生活。

3
我最想念的
What I Miss Most · "Say hi to Via for me."
想念电话

One of the things Miranda missed most about Via's friendship was her family. "I loved her mom and dad. They were always so welcoming and nice to me. I knew they loved their kids more than anything. I always felt safe around them: safer than anywhere else in the world." And she loved Auggie. She was never afraid of him. Her other friends couldn't believe she went to Via's house. "His face creeps me out." "You're stupid. Auggie's face isn't so bad once you get used to it."

She called Via's house once, just to say hello to Auggie. Maybe part of her hoped Via would answer. "Hey, Major Tom!" Auggie sounded so happy to hear her voice. He told her excitedly about his new school, his friends Jack and Summer. She told him high school was different — you end up hanging out with loads of different people. "Well, I was just calling to tell you I miss you and hope you're having a good year. Feel free to call me whenever you want, okay, Auggie? You know I love you always." "I love you, too, Miranda!" "Say hi to Via for me." "I will. Bye!"

✦ Major Tom 的电话

这个电话是全书最被低估的场景之一。Miranda 打电话只是为了听见 Auggie 的声音——她无法直接联系 Via,但通过他,她回到了那个"更安全"的家庭。她说"你随时可以打给我",但真正需要打电话的人是她自己。

4
非凡却无人看见
Extraordinary, but No One There to See · "Why are you doing this, Miranda?"
牺牲选择

Neither of Miranda's parents could come to opening night. Zack had a volleyball game. Her "friends" all went to the game. Even Ella chose the crowd. "So on opening night no one that was remotely close to me was even there."

But she had discovered she was good at this. Really good. "I felt the part. I understood the words I spoke. I could read the lines as if they were coming from my brain and my heart. And on opening night, I can honestly say I knew I was going to be more than good: I was going to be great. I was going to be extraordinary, but there would be no one there to see."

She peeked through the curtain. That's when she saw Auggie walking down the aisle with Isabel and Nate. He was wearing a bow tie, looking around excitedly. He had grown up. His hair was shorter. He had hearing aids now. His face hadn't changed a bit. "Mr. Davenport," she said, surprising herself. "I'm sorry, but I can't go on tonight."

Davenport was furious. She faked being sick. He screamed for Via. In the dressing room, Via asked: "Why are you doing this, Miranda?" She never got to answer. She wasn't sure what the answer was.

✦ 聚光灯的转让

Miranda 在那一刻做了一件几乎不可能的事:她知道自己是 Emily Gibbs 的最佳人选,她知道这会是她最辉煌的夜晚——但她看到了台下穿蝴蝶结领结的 Auggie,她选择了把自己最闪耀的时刻让给 Via。这不是因为"不舒服",而是因为她在那一秒明白了有些人比舞台更需要这个时刻。

5
演出
The Performance · "Good, good, good."
戏剧旁观

Miranda watched the rest of the play from the wings, next to Davenport. Justin was amazing. Via, in the heartbreaking last scene, was awesome — one flubbed line, Justin covered for her, nobody noticed. "I heard Davenport muttering under his breath: 'Good, good, good.'" The only moment of regret — bittersweet, she called it — was the curtain call when the audience rose to their feet for Via and Justin. But just a few minutes later she saw Nate, Isabel, and Auggie make their way backstage. Everyone was congratulating the actors. In the crush of people, she noticed Auggie looking kind of lost. She cut through the crowd as fast as she could. "Hey! Major Tom!"

✦ 侧台的 Miranda

Miranda 从主演变成了侧台旁观者——但她没有离开。她没有回家,没有去派对,而是站在幕布旁边,看着她的角色在另一个人身上发光。当她说"bittersweet"时,这个词装着她一整年的孤独。

6
演出之后
After the Show · "For the first time in a very, very long time, I felt absolutely happy."
回归家庭

August hugged Miranda tight. "I can't believe how big you've gotten." "I thought you were going to be in the play!" "I wasn't up to it. But Via was great, don't you think?" Isabel found them and kissed Miranda's cheek — exactly like she always used to. Nate invited her to the late-night dinner celebrating the show. "Pleeease?" said Auggie. Via put her arm around Miranda. "You're definitely coming." She was smiling her old smile. They started leading her out of the crowd, and I have to admit, for the first time in a very, very long time, I felt absolutely happy.

✦ 旧的微笑

故事的最后一句属于 Miranda:"For the first time in a very, very long time, I felt absolutely happy."她不是主角,不是 Emily Gibbs,不是任何人的明星——但她回到了一个愿意接纳她的家庭。Via 的"旧的微笑"是全书的治愈:朋友可以走散,但真正的门永远开着。
