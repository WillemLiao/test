Wonder段十二 · Via 的新生活 · 万圣节后的对话

R. J. Palacio
Via 找到了新朋友——聪明人餐桌的 Eleanor 和 Justin 让她看到了新的可能。万圣节那天,她因为想念 Grans 而哭泣,和妈妈短暂地重新连接。但 August 被提前接回了家。她最终说服 Auggie 回到学校,并得知 Miranda 给 August 打过电话——"告诉她我 miss 她"。
1
告别旧友
Out with the Old · Eleanor and Justin
新朋友成长

Miranda and Ella blasted off. They attached themselves to a new crowd. After a week of painful lunches, I decided to make a clean break. They asked no questions. I told no lies. We just went our separate ways.

I finished War and Peace in October. I ended up at the smart-kids' table with Eleanor — a girl from PS 22 I'd never realized was so funny. She'd never liked Miranda and Ella. She thought they were stuck-up.

The table included Eleanor's boyfriend Kevin, a few techie guys, and a quiet guy named Justin who played the violin and who I had an instant crush on. When I'd see Miranda and Ella, we'd say "Hey, what's up," and move on. Occasionally Miranda would ask about August. This I never told him about.

✦ 聪明人餐桌

Via 从受欢迎圈子走向了更真实的圈子——而且遇到了让她心动的小提琴手 Justin。

2
10月31日
October 31 · "Via's mom" put away again
Grans失落

Grans had died the night before Halloween. This has always been a sad time of year for me. Mom immerses herself in August's costume. I won't mention the fact that Mom has never made any of my costumes, because it has no bearing on anything at all.

The morning of Halloween I woke up thinking about Grans. Dad kept yelling to hurry up, I started crying. So Dad took August to school, and Mom said I could stay home. The two of us cried together. It felt good to cry with Mom. We were about to watch The Ghost and Mrs. Muir — our favorite — when the phone rang. The nurse from August's school: he had a stomach ache. Mom went to pick him up. "Via's mom" was put away again.

✦ "Via 的妈妈"收起来了

短暂的母女时光被 August 的中断打断了。Via 没有抱怨——但"收起来了"这个词道尽了她的无奈。

3
不给糖就捣蛋
Trick or Treat · "Come on, Auggie"
兄妹安慰

August said he wasn't feeling well enough to go trick-or-treating. I knew how much he loved it — the one night a year when he could be like every other kid. No one knew he was different under the mask. To August, that must have felt absolutely amazing.

At seven I knocked on his door. He was just lying in bed, staring at the ceiling. The Bleeding Scream costume was crumpled on the floor next to the Boba Fett costume Mom had made. He told me what happened. He had overheard mean things. One of the boys was his "best friend" Jack Will.

"Sometimes kids are stupid," I said. "Then why would he say it? Tushman probably bribed him!" He started punching his pillow: "I hate it! I hate it! I hate it!"

I convinced him to go to the parade. "Come on, Auggie. Let's just go. It'll be fun. And I'll let you have all my candy." He put on his Boba Fett costume. By the time he put his helmet on, he was feeling better.

✦ 姐姐的力量

Via 是唯一能让 Auggie 走出房间的人。她用自己的方式——糖果、耐心和不放弃——把他从绝望中拉了出来。

4
思考时间
Time to Think · "She said to tell you she misses you"
对话Miranda

August faked a stomach ache on Friday too. By Sunday he was still determined not to go back. I pulled the comic book out of his hands: "Are you really going to let a couple of stupid kids keep you from going back to school?"

"Do people go out of their way to avoid touching you, Via? Right. So don't compare your bad days to mine." "Okay, that's fair. But it's not a contest about whose days suck the most. The point is we all have to put up with bad days."

He brought up Miranda. "Why do you keep bringing Miranda up?" I yelled. "She called the other day," he said. "She was calling me. She said she'd always love me like a big sister."

I was stunned. I threatened to tell Mom and Dad if he didn't go back. "Fine! I'll go! Just stop talking about it already." As I turned to leave, I asked: "Did Miranda say anything else about me?" "She said to tell you she misses you. Quote unquote." I nodded, too embarrassed to let him see how happy that made me.

✦ "她 miss 你"

Via 最终用激将法说服了 Auggie。而 Miranda 那句"告诉她我 miss 她",为两条叙事线的交汇埋下了伏笔。
