Via 上高中了——她终于可以不用再当"那个弟弟很丑的姐姐"。但最好的两个朋友 Miranda 和 Ella 今年变得很陌生。Miranda 染了粉色头发、换了风格,像变了一个人。Via 的宇宙在多个层面同时震动。

高中
High School · "Olivia, not Via"
新开始身份

What I always loved most about middle school was that it was separate from home. I could go there and be Olivia Pullman — not Via, which is my name at home.

In elementary school, everyone knew all about us. August was a fixture — Mom brought him to all my plays and concerts. The janitor knew him. But in middle school, a lot of people didn't know about August. Or if they knew, it wasn't the first thing they knew about me. "Olivia? Yeah, she's nice. Did you hear she has a brother who's deformed?" I always hated that word.

The best thing about high school is hardly anybody knows me at all. Except Miranda and Ella. They've known August since he was a baby. We were so happy when we all got into Faulkner High. But this year, nothing is like I thought it would be.

✦ Olivia 与 Via

Via 在学校叫 Olivia——她想摆脱那个永远和 August 绑在一起的标签。这不是背叛,而是她在寻找自己。

2
Major Tom
Major Tom · "Space Oddity"
Miranda疏远

Out of the three of us, Miranda had almost always been the sweetest to August. She gave him the astronaut helmet he wore every day when he was five. She called him Major Tom and they sang "Space Oddity" together.

Since Miranda's always been good about calling after summer camp, I was surprised when I didn't hear from her. Then I saw from Facebook she'd been home for two weeks. On the first day of school, I was shocked. Miranda's hair was cut in a super-cute bob dyed bright pink, and she was wearing a tube top totally not her style.

But it wasn't just the look: she was acting differently. Distant. Like I was a casual friend. It was obvious Ella and Miranda had gotten together during the summer without me. I pretended not to be upset, but I could feel my face getting hot, my smile being fake. I had always thought I was above this kind of teenage pettiness, but I felt a lump in my throat throughout lunch.

✦ 粉红色的头发

Miranda 的外表变了,但更让人难过的是她的距离感。Via 被两个最好的朋友无声地排除在外。

3
放学后
After School · "How was your first day?"
家庭谎言

Miranda offered to drive me home. "You don't have to. My mom can pick me up." I lied — I couldn't sit in a car with the new Miranda. After school I ducked into a restroom, then took the subway home by myself.

Mom asked how my day was. "We stopped for pizza." Incredible how easily a lie can slip through your lips. She asked about Miranda. "She went straight home. We have a lot of homework." "On your first day?" "Yes!" I yelled, which completely surprised her. Then I asked about Auggie's day to change the subject.

I stormed into Auggie's room. He was on his PlayStation, didn't look up. I asked how school was. "Fine." "Were people nice?" "Yes!" He looked at me like I'd asked the dumbest question in the world. It was the first time I heard him be sarcastic.

✦ 善意的谎言

Via 对妈妈撒谎,就像 Auggie 对 Via 撒谎——这个家庭的每个成员都在用自己的方式保护彼此。

学徒辫的终结
The Padawan Bites the Dust · Losing the braid
兄妹父亲

I'm not sure when Auggie cut off his Padawan braid, or why that made me really mad. He had always been so proud of it. He and Christopher grew their braids together. When August cut it off without explanation, I was just so upset I can't explain why.

I've seen Auggie brushing his hair in the bathroom mirror, tilting his head to look from different angles — like there's some magic perspective inside the mirror that could change the dimensions of his face.

That night Mom didn't come back to check on me — she fell asleep in Auggie's room. Dad came instead. "Don't be mad at Mom. You know how much she worries about Auggie." "I know." He asked about my day. I told him Miranda and Ella were acting like jerks. He said nothing tests friendships like high school, then poked fun at my reading War and Peace. It was silly stuff, but Dad always managed to make everyone laugh. And sometimes that's all you need.

✦ 爸爸的幽默

妈妈又一次没能遵守承诺,但爸爸的俏皮话让一切好起来。这个家庭的爱以不同的方式表现——妈妈的担忧和爸爸的幽默。
