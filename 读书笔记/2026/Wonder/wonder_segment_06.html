夏天的餐桌
The Summer Table · "Our names kind of match"
友谊温暖

"Hey, is this seat taken?" A girl with long wavy brown hair stood across from my table. She put her lunch tray down, plopped her backpack on the floor, and sat down across from me.

"My name is Summer, by the way. What's yours?"

"August."

"Cool."

Another girl came over: "Why are you sitting here? Come back to the table." Summer said it was too crowded. The other girl looked confused and left.

"Hey, our names kind of match," Summer said. "Summer? August?" "We can make this the 'summer only' lunch table. Only kids with summer names can sit here."

She started writing a list of names. We found ways to connect every name to summer, even Jack Will: "Jack will go to the beach."

"But if someone doesn't have a summer name and wants to sit with us," she said very seriously, "we'll still let them if they're nice, okay?"

"Okay."

Summer looked like her name. She had a tan, and her eyes were green like a leaf.

✦ Summer 与 August

两个季节的名字,一段最温暖的友谊。Summer 的善良不需要任何理由——她只是坐下来,就这么简单。

2
从一到十
One to Ten · "Beauty and the Beast"
母子打分

Mom always had a habit of asking how something felt on a scale of one to ten. It started after my jaw surgery, when I couldn't talk because my mouth was wired shut. She'd point to a bandage and I'd hold up fingers.

When school ended, Mom was waiting at the front entrance. "So, how was it? One to ten?"

"Five," I said, shrugging. She was surprised. "Wow, that's even better than I hoped for."

We walked through the crowd. Kids were pointing me out to each other. I walked in front of Mom through the crowd. "See you tomorrow, August!" — Summer waved from across the street.

Mom asked a million questions. I was kind of mad at her for no reason. "She's very pretty," Mom said. "Yeah, I know. We're kind of like Beauty and the Beast."

I didn't wait to see Mom's reaction. I just started running after a rock I kicked as hard as I could.

✦ 1到10分

给第一天打 5 分——不算好,但也没那么糟。而"美女与野兽"的自嘲,是 Auggie 用幽默掩盖的苦涩。

3
学徒辫
Padawan · "Why do I have to be so ugly?"
崩溃母爱

That night I cut off the little braid on the back of my head. Dad noticed first: "Oh good, I never liked that thing." Via couldn't believe it: "That took you years to grow!"

Dad tucked me in later. We talked about the day. "It was better than okay, actually." He asked if I was mad at Mom. "No, she's more to blame. It was her idea."

Mom came to say goodnight. She started reading The Hobbit aloud. "'They were a gloomy party that night, and the gloom gathered still deeper on them in the following days.' I'm not sure why, but all of a sudden I started to cry.

Mom put the book down and wrapped her arms around me. She didn't seem surprised. "Why do I have to be so ugly, Mommy?" I whispered. "No, baby, you're not …"

She kissed me all over my face. She kissed my eyes that came down too far. She kissed my cheeks that looked punched in. She kissed my tortoise mouth.

She said soft words that I know were meant to help me, but words can't change my face.

✦ "我为什么这么丑"

Auggie 卸下了一整天的坚强。妈妈亲吻他的每一寸不完美的脸——这个画面比任何安慰都更有力量。

4
九月结束时叫醒我
Wake Me Up when September Ends · Normalization
适应时间

The rest of September was hard. I wasn't used to getting up early, or homework, or quizzes. I never got quizzes when Mom homeschooled me.

Every new class was a new chance for kids to "not stare" at me. They would sneak peeks from behind their notebooks. They would take the longest way around me, like I had some germ they could catch, like my face was contagious.

In the hallways, my face would surprise some unsuspecting kid. They'd make a little "uh!" sound. This happened maybe four or five times a day for the first few weeks.

I kind of wanted to tell them: it's okay, I know I'm weird-looking, take a look, I don't bite. Hey, the truth is, if a Wookiee started going to the school all of a sudden, I'd probably stare a bit!

It took about one week for kids in my class to get used to my face. Two weeks for the rest of my grade. About a month for the whole school. Some of them had earrings in their noses. Some of them had pimples. None of them looked like me.

✦ 适应的节奏

Auggie 用 Wookiee 的比喻来理解别人的目光——他没有愤怒,只有理解。时间是最好的良药。
