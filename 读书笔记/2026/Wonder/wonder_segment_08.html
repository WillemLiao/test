Wonder段八 · 万圣节的背叛 · 第一部分终章

R. J. Palacio
拍照恐惧症、"奶酪碰触"的孤立——然后到了万圣节。Auggie 临时换了 costume，却无意中听到了他最信任的朋友 Jack 说出的最残忍的话。第一部分在他的崩溃中落幕：他再也不想回学校了。
1
学校照相日
School Pictures · "I have an aversion"
恐惧照片

I don't think anyone will be shocked to learn I don't want to have my school picture taken on October 22. No way. No thank you. I stopped letting anyone take pictures of me a while ago. I guess you could call it a phobia. No, it's an "aversion," which is a word I just learned in Mr. Browne's class.

I thought Mom would try to get me to drop my aversion, but she didn't. Unfortunately, I couldn't get out of the class picture. The photographer looked like he'd just sucked on a lemon when he saw me. I'm sure he thought I ruined the picture. I didn't smile, not that anyone could tell if I had.

✦ 不情愿的照片

摄影师的表情比任何辱骂都更直接地告诉 Auggie:你"毁了"这张照片。连专业人士都无法掩饰的反应,是最真实的伤害。

2
奶酪碰触
The Cheese Touch · "I'm the old moldy cheese"
孤立比喻

I noticed that even though people were getting used to me, no one would actually touch me. In dance class, Mrs. Atanabi tried to make Ximena Chin be my partner. I've never seen someone have a panic attack before, but I'm pretty sure Ximena had one. She got pale and literally broke into a sweat.

In science, we were huddled around heating plates. Seven kids were squished on one side while I had loads of room on the other. Ms. Rubin made Tristan and Nino come to my side. When my hand accidentally bumped Tristan's for a fraction of a second, he jerked his hand away so fast he dropped his foil on the floor, then ran to the lab sink to wash his hands as fast as possible.

I think it's like the Cheese Touch in Diary of a Wimpy Kid. At Beecher Prep, I'm the old moldy cheese.

✦ 旧奶酪

Auggie 把自己比作《小屁孩日记》里的旧奶酪——这个比喻精准又心酸。没人想碰他,哪怕一毫秒都不行。

3
万圣节服装
Costumes · "I wish every day could be Halloween"
万圣节面具

For me, Halloween is the best holiday in the world. I get to dress up in a costume. I get to wear a mask. Nobody takes a second look. Nobody notices me. Nobody knows me. I wish every day could be Halloween. We could all wear masks all the time. Then we could walk around and get to know each other before we got to see what we looked like under the masks.

This year I was going to be Boba Fett. Mom painted a Jango Fett costume green to make it look worn. Julian was going as Jango Fett — a weird coincidence. He didn't like hearing I was Boba.

On Halloween morning, Via had a meltdown. Dad needed to go. I rushed to put on my costume, but suddenly I didn't feel like wearing it. At the last minute, I threw on the Bleeding Scream costume from last year — just a black robe and a big white mask.

✦ 面具的哲学

"我希望每天都是万圣节"——这是 Auggie 最令人心碎的愿望。面具让他不再被注视,让他终于能做一个普通的孩子。

4
血淋淋的尖叫
The Bleeding Scream · "I'd kill myself"
背叛心碎

Walking through the halls that morning was absolutely awesome. I walked with my head up. A kid in the same mask high-fived me. I wondered if he would have ever done that if he'd known it was me under the mask.

Then I got to homeroom. The first costume I saw was Darth Sidious — Julian. He was talking to two mummies. I walked over to a desk near them.

One mummy said: "What he really looks like is one of those shrunken heads." "I think he looks like an orc." Julian's voice: "If I looked like that, I swear to God, I'd put a hood over my face every day."

"I've thought about this a lot," said the second mummy. "I really think … if I looked like him, seriously, I think that I'd kill myself."

I knew the voice. It was Jack.

"Then why do you hang out with him so much?" asked Julian. "Tushman asked me to hang out with him. The thing is: he always follows me around. What am I supposed to do?"

I walked out of the class without anyone knowing I'd been there. I started crying. The tears were so thick in my eyes I could barely see, but I couldn't wipe them through the mask. I wanted a hole I could fall inside of: a little black hole that would eat me up.

✦ 最深的背叛

在万圣节的面具下,Auggie 听到了 Jack 最真实的声音。原来自己以为的友情,只是别人被要求的任务——这是全书最残酷的一幕。

5
绰号
Names · "I'm never going back to school again"
崩溃逃避

Rat boy. Freak. Monster. Freddy Krueger. E.T. Gross-out. Lizard face. Mutant. I know the names they call me. I've been in enough playgrounds to know kids can be mean. I know, I know, I know.

I ended up in the second-floor bathroom. I locked the door, took off my mask, and cried for I don't know how long. Then I went to the nurse's office and said I had a stomach ache — which was true, because I felt like I'd been kicked in the gut.

Mom came. She helped me walk home. I didn't tell her anything. When she asked if I felt well enough to go trick-or-treating, I said no. I heard her say to Dad on the phone: "He doesn't even have the energy to go trick-or-treating. … Imagine his missing Halloween."

I got out of school the next day, too — Friday. So I had the whole weekend to think. I was pretty sure I would never go back to school again.
