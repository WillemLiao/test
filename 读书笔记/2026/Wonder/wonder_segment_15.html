Wonder段十五 · Jack 的视角 · 真正的朋友

R. J. Palacio
Jack 发现自己真的喜欢和 August 做朋友——他有趣、聪明、讲义气。但万圣节那天的话让两人的友谊破裂。August 开始冷落 Jack,而 Jack 完全摸不着头脑——直到 Summer 在他耳边说了两个字:"Bleeding Scream"。
1
首先
First of All · "I would choose August"
友情真心

First of all, you do get used to his face. After about a week, I was like, huh, it's not so bad.

Second of all, he's actually a really cool dude. He's pretty funny. The teacher will say something and August will whisper something funny to me that no one else hears and totally make me crack up.

Third of all, he's really smart. He lets me cheat off of him if I really need to. He also let me copy his homework once. When we got caught, August lied to protect me: "Oh, that's because we did our homework together last night."

"After we left the classroom, I said: 'Dude, thanks for doing that.' And he was like, 'No problem.' That was cool."

Fourthly, now that I know him, I would say I actually do want to be friends with August. "At first, I admit it, I was only friendly to him because Mr. Tushman asked me to be especially nice. But now I would choose to hang out with him. I kind of feel like I can tell August anything. Like he's a good friend. If all the guys in fifth grade were lined up against a wall and I got to choose anyone I wanted to hang out with, I would choose August."

✦ 我会选他

Jack 用最朴实的方式定义了这段友谊——如果可以选任何人,我选 August。不是因为同情,不是因为任务,而是因为他真的是个好朋友。

2
血淋淋的尖叫
Bleeding Scream · "It's all August's fault"
困惑冷战

"Bleeding Scream? What the heck? Summer Dawson has always been a bit out there, but this was too much."

August was ignoring Jack and Jack had no idea why. "It's so weird because one day, me and August were friends. And the next day, whoosh, he was hardly talking to me." All Summer would say was "Bleeding Scream."

Jack started ignoring him back. "I've started ignoring him like he's ignoring me in school now. This is actually kind of hard since we sit next to each other in practically every class."

Without August, Jack could hang out with more kids. "Why this is bad is because, well, (a) I don't actually enjoy hanging out with the popular group that much. And (b) I actually liked hanging out with August. So this is kind of messed up. And it's all August's fault."

✦ 都是他的错

Jack 的自我辩解充满了孩子气的逻辑——他在生 August 的气,但他真正气的是自己不知道自己做错了什么。

3
第一场雪
The First Snow · Lightning
治愈雪橇

"The first snow of winter hit right before Thanksgiving break. School was closed, so we got an extra day of vacation. I was glad about that because I was so bummed about this whole August thing."

Jack loved snow days — that quiet morning, running to the window, everything covered in white. "I'm never going to be one of those grown-ups that use an umbrella when it's snowing—ever."

His dad took him and Jamie sledding. On the way home, Jack found a broken wooden sled propped against a monument. He fixed it up — super-glued the slats, wrapped duct tape, spray-painted it white, and painted LIGHTNING in gold letters. His dad was impressed: "Wow, Jackie! You were right about the sled!"

The next day, Lightning was the fastest thing Jack had ever ridden. By the end of the weekend, the snow was gone. "It was rainy and yucky the first day back from vacation. A slushy day. That's how I was feeling inside, too. I nodded 'hey' to August the first time I saw him. I wanted to tell him about Lightning, but I didn't."

✦ 闪电

Jack 亲手修理的雪橇叫"闪电",但他最想分享这件事的人却不跟他说话了。那个说不出口的故事比雪橇本身更让人难过。

4
十二月的格言
December Precept · "Fortune favors the bold"
勇气反思

"Mr. Browne's December precept was: Fortune favors the bold. We were all supposed to write a paragraph about some time in our lives when we did something very brave."

"I have to say that I think the bravest thing I ever did was become friends with August. But I couldn't write about that, of course. I was afraid we'd have to read these out loud. So, instead, I wrote this lame thing about how I used to be afraid of the ocean. It was dumb."

"I wonder what August wrote about. He probably had a lot of things to choose from."

✦ 最勇敢的事

Jack 认为和 August 做朋友是自己做过最勇敢的事——但他不敢写出来。这个细节完美地概括了 Jack 的困境:他在乎 August,但也太在乎别人怎么看他。
