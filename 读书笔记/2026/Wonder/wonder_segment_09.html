1
银河系漫游
A Tour of the Galaxy · "August is the Sun"
比喻孤独

August is the Sun. Me and Mom and Dad are planets orbiting the Sun. The rest of our family and friends are asteroids and comets floating around the planets orbiting the Sun.

I'm used to the way this universe works. If I wanted Mom and Dad to watch me play soccer, nine out of ten times they'd miss it because they were busy shuttling August to speech therapy or physical therapy or a new specialist or a surgery.

Mom and Dad would always say I was the most understanding little girl in the world. After you've seen August after his surgeries — his little face bandaged up, his tiny body full of IVs — it feels crazy to complain about not getting a toy you wanted. I knew this even when I was six. No one told me. I just knew it.

So I've gotten used to not complaining. I've done every school project by myself. My worst day, worst fall, worst headache, worst bruise, worst mean thing anyone could say has always been nothing compared to what August has gone through. This isn't me being noble: it's just the way I know it is.

But this year there seems to be a shift in the cosmos. The galaxy is changing. Planets are falling out of alignment.

✦ 宇宙的秩序

Via 用"太阳系"来合理化自己被忽视的童年。但今年,星系正在改变——她和 August 都在经历各自的成长阵痛。

2
在 August 之前
Before August · "It doesn't look like Lilly!"
回忆妹妹

I honestly don't remember my life before August came into it. I look at pictures of me as a baby — Mom and Dad smiling so happily, holding me. Dad was this hipster dude and Mom was this cute Brazilian fashionista. You can see in that picture how I really was the first child, first grandchild, first niece.

Everyone has a story about the day they brought August home. Apparently, I just looked at him for a long time, then said: "It doesn't look like Lilly!" — Lilly was the lifelike doll Grans had given me to "practice" being a big sister. After my initial reaction, it only took a few minutes before I was all over him: kissing him, cuddling him. I never so much as touched or mentioned Lilly ever again.

✦ 从莉莉到 August

Via 把娃娃莉莉完全抛在一边——她从一开始就毫无保留地爱着这个与众不同的弟弟。

3
看见 August
Seeing August · "You are my everything"
觉醒Grans

I never used to see August the way other people saw him. I'd just get mad when they stared. "What the heck are you looking at?" I'd say to people — even grown-ups.

When I was eleven, I stayed with Grans in Montauk for four weeks while August had his big jaw surgery. It was so amazing to suddenly be free — no one stared at us, no one pointed. I told Grans I wished I could live with her forever.

Coming home felt strange. For a tiny fraction of a moment, I saw August not the way I've always seen him, but the way other people see him. All I could see was the drool coming down his chin. And suddenly I was like all those people who would stare or look away.

It only lasted a second, but it had opened a peephole for me. On the other side there were two Augusts: the one I saw blindly, and the one other people saw.

Two months later, Grans died. A heart attack, out of the blue. On my last day in Montauk, she told me a secret: she loved me more than anyone else in the world. "Even August?" I had asked. She smiled: "But he has many angels looking out for him already. I want you to know that you have me looking out for you." After she died, I held on to that secret like a blanket.

✦ 窥视孔

Via 第一次从别人的眼睛看弟弟——这让愧疚伴随了她多年。Grans 的那句"你是我的一切"是她心中最温暖的光。

4
从窥视孔看 August
August Through the Peephole · The face described
描述成长

His eyes are about an inch below where they should be on his face. They slant downward like diagonal slits. They bulge outward because his eye cavities are too shallow. He doesn't have eyebrows or eyelashes. His nose is disproportionately big. His head is pinched in on the sides where the ears should be. He has a severe overbite and an extremely undersized jawbone.

Does August see how other people see him? When he looks in the mirror, does he see the Auggie Mom and Dad see, or the Auggie everyone else sees?

Here's what I think: we've all spent so much time trying to make August think he's normal that he actually thinks he is normal. And the problem is, he's not.

He used to be easier to read before the surgeries. He looks better now, but the signs we used to gauge his moods are gone. There's a part of me that doesn't want to keep trying: why can't he just say what he's feeling like everyone else? He's ten years old. He can use his words.

✦ 独角戏

Via 坦诚地承认了自己的疲惫——她一直在围绕 August 的情绪行事,而这份疲惫从未有人问过。
