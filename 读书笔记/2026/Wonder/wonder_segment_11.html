Wonder段十一 · 基因与门外的身影 · 隐形的姐姐

R. J. Palacio
深夜,妈妈站在 August 门外,像幽灵一样聆听他的呼吸。Via 在早餐时爆发,质问为什么妈妈答应回来看她却没来。然后,她冷静地讲述了一家人的基因图谱——以及她自己身体里也携带着那条突变基因。
1
门前的幽灵
An Apparition at the Door · "Is Auggie okay?"
母亲夜晚

Once, I got up in the middle of the night because I was thirsty. I saw Mom standing outside Auggie's room. Her hand was on the doorknob, her forehead leaning on the door, which was ajar. She wasn't going in or stepping out — just standing outside the door, as if she was listening to the sound of his breathing as he slept.

The hallway lights were out. The only thing illuminating her was the blue night-light in August's bedroom. She looked ghostlike. Or maybe I should say angelic.

"Is Auggie okay?" I asked. "Oh, he's fine," she said, wrapping her arms around me. She walked me back to my room and kissed me good night. She never explained what she was doing outside his door.

I wonder how many nights she's stood outside his door. And I wonder if she's ever stood outside my door like that.

✦ 天使还是幽灵？

这个画面无比温柔又无比心痛。Via 用一个问题道尽了所有被忽视的感受:她有没有也站在过我的门外？

2
早餐
Breakfast · "You would know if you had come back"
家庭争吵独立

The next morning I asked Mom to pick me up from school instead of Miranda's mother. "No, Mom! You pick me up. Or I'll just take the subway." "You know I don't want you to take the subway by yourself yet."

Dad stepped in: "She's old enough to take the subway by herself."

Mom looked at both of us. "Is something going on?" "You would know if you had come back to check on me, like you said you would." Mom remembered how she'd ditched me. She put down the knife, apologized. I let her off the hook. But when she offered to pick me up with Auggie in the car, I said no — I didn't want August at my new school.

Dad sided with me: "She's reading War and Peace, for crying out loud!" He and I jogged down the stoop together. Mom yelled from the window about calling before the subway. Dad walked backward, smiling: "War and Peace, Isabel! War and Peace!"

✦ 爸爸的同盟

爸爸是 Via 的坚定支持者。他用幽默和信任给了她独立的空间——"她在读《战争与和平》啊！"

3
基因学入门
Genetics 101 · "I carry it, too"
家族科学

Both sides of Dad's family were Jews from Russia and Poland. Mom's family is from Brazil. Not one person in the exotic mix of my family gene pool has ever shown any signs of having what August has. I've pored over old photos — grainy sepia pictures, black-and-white snapshots — not once did I detect even a trace of August's face.

After August was born, my parents underwent genetic counseling. The doctors found a "previously unknown type of mandibulofacial dysostosis" caused by a mutation in the TCOF1 gene. The weird thing is, though you'd never know it from looking at them: both my parents carry that mutant gene.

And I carry it, too.

✦ 隐藏的基因

Via 也携带那条基因。她翻遍老照片寻找线索——但基因的奥秘在于,它看不见,却决定了August 的一切。

4
庞尼特方格
The Punnett Square · "Countless babies who'll never be born, like mine"
概率未来

If I have children, there's a one-in-two chance I'll pass on the defective gene. If I marry someone with the same gene, there's a one-in-four chance our kids will look like August. If August has children with someone who doesn't have the gene, his kids will all carry the gene but look normal.

Countless doctors have drawn tic-tac-toe grids for my parents over the years. Geneticists use Punnett squares to determine inheritance, probabilities and chance. But for all they know, there's more they don't know.

I actually like how doctors talk. I like the sound of science. I like how words you don't understand explain things you can't understand. There are countless people under words like "germline mosaicism," "chromosome rearrangement," or "delayed mutation." Countless babies who'll never be born, like mine.

✦ 科学的诗意

Via 冷静地分析着概率的冷酷。最后一句话揭示了她内心最深处的恐惧:有一天,她可能也要面对同样的选择。
