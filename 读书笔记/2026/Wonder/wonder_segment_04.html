1
约定
The Deal · "Everyone was really nice"
告别善意

Mom and Mr. Tushman were talking when we got back to the office. Mrs. Garcia smiled her shiny smile. Jack, Julian, and Charlotte stood by the door, not sure where to go.

Mom asked if I saw the baby chicks in science. Julian answered for me. Then Mr. Tushman asked if the tour was enough. Charlotte remembered the art room. "Don't we have to pick Via up soon?" I said to Mom. That was our signal — my way of saying I wanted to leave.

Mom pretended to check her watch. "We have to go pick up my daughter at her new school." It was a lie — Via was coming home with Dad later. But Mr. Tushman seemed surprised we were leaving so suddenly. I wondered if he would blame Jack and Charlotte, even though it was really only Julian who made me feel kind of bad.

"Everyone was really nice," I made sure to tell Mr. Tushman before we left.

"Bye," I said to Jack, Charlotte, and Julian, but I didn't look at them — or look up at all — until I left the building.

✦ 懂事的孩子

即使受了委屈，Auggie 还是告诉 Mr. Tushman"每个人都很友好"——他不想让任何人因为自己而受到责怪。

2
回家之后
Home · "I want to"
转折勇气

As soon as we'd walked half a block from the school, Mom asked how it went. "Not yet, Mom. When we get home."

The moment we got inside, I ran to my room and threw myself on my bed. I felt very sad and a tiny bit happy at the exact same time. My dog Daisy jumped on the bed and started licking my face.

"Was that boy Julian nice?" Mom asked. "Oh, no, he was the least nice. I liked Jack, though. He was nice."

"He was like, 'So, August, what's the deal with your face?'" I said, looking at Daisy the whole time. "Were you in a fire or something?"

Mom didn't say anything. Her cheeks were bright red. "I'm so sorry, Auggie," she said quietly.

"No, it's okay, Mom, really."

"You don't have to go to school if you don't want, sweetie."

"I want to," I said. "Really, Mom. I want to." And I wasn't lying.

✦ 孩子的决定

妈妈红着脸道歉，但 Auggie 说"我想去"——不是出于勇敢，而是因为 Jack 的微笑让他看到了希望。

3
开学紧张症
First-Day Jitters · "Everyone's just as nervous as you"
开学家庭

On the first day of school, the butterflies in my stomach were more like pigeons flying around my insides. Up until a few days before, we still weren't sure I would be going. After the tour, Mom and Dad had reversed sides. Dad was now the one saying I should go and Mom was the one saying I shouldn't.

Dad told me he was really proud of how I'd handled myself with Julian. But Mom wasn't so sure anymore. She seemed relieved that the whole family would walk me to school together.

Even though Beecher Prep is just a few blocks from our house, I've only been on that block a couple of times. In general, I try to avoid blocks where there are lots of kids roaming around. On our block, everybody knows me. I know every brick and every tree trunk and every crack in the sidewalk.

As we turned the corner, we saw hundreds of kids in front of the school. I kept my head way down. "Everyone's just as nervous as you are," said Via in my ear. "Just remember that this is everyone's first day of school."

I didn't catch anyone staring at first. Only once did I see some girls looking my way and whispering, but they looked away when I noticed. Mom hugged me — I could tell she was about to cry — so I gave her a fast hard hug, turned, and disappeared into the school.

✦ 家人的角色反转

父母在"保护"和"放手"之间摇摆。参观后，轮到爸爸推他向前、妈妈想保护他了。Via 在耳边的叮嘱是最温柔的鼓励。

4
密码锁
Locks · "Hi, August"
教室友谊

I went straight to room 301. I was glad I'd gone on that tour — I knew exactly where to go. I chose the desk in the middle toward the back. No one sat next to me. A couple of times someone was about to, then changed their mind at the last minute.

Charlotte waved from the front. Julian didn't say hello. Then suddenly someone sat down next to me. It was Jack Will. "What's up," he said, nodding.

Ms. Petosa, the teacher, wrote her name on the board. She noticed me — only hesitated for a millionth of a second, but I could tell the moment she saw me. Like I said: I'm used to it by now.

She called attendance and handed out folders with combination locks. The seat next to mine was the only empty one. She pointed a big kid named Henry Joplin to sit there. He did not want to move next to me. He plopped his backpack up really high on the right side of the desk so it was like a wall between us.

When Ms. Petosa called "August Pullman," I went up to get my folder. I felt everyone's eyes burning into my back. But she smiled at me very nicely. She looked like Mon Mothma from Star Wars Episode IV. Henry couldn't open his lock and got frustrated. The funny thing is, if he hadn't put the backpack between us, I would have offered to help him.

✦ 书包的墙

Henry 用背包在桌上筑起了一道墙——不需要语言，比任何话语都清楚地表达了"我不想靠近你"。但 Jack 主动坐在他旁边，形成了最鲜明的对比。
