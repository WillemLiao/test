R. J. Palacio
参观正式开始。Mrs. Garcia 用两种微笑迎接他。三个同龄人——Jack、Julian、Charlotte——被安排带他参观学校。Julian 的问题越来越刺人，但 Jack Will 的一个微笑让 Auggie 觉得，也许一切没那么糟。
1
好心的 Mrs. Garcia
Nice Mrs. Garcia · "The kid's got a killer handshake"
初次见面善意

WE FOLLOWED MR. TUSHMAN down a few hallways. There weren't a lot of people around. And the few people who were there didn't seem to notice me at all, though that may have been because they didn't see me. I sort of hid behind Mom as I walked.

We ended up in a small room with the words OFFICE OF THE MIDDLE SCHOOL DIRECTOR on the door. Inside sat a nice-seeming lady behind a desk — Mrs. Garcia. Mom stepped aside so I would move forward. Then that thing happened that I've seen happen a million times before. When I looked up at her, Mrs. Garcia's eyes dropped for a second. It was so fast no one else would have noticed. She was smiling a really shiny smile.

"Such a pleasure to meet you, August," she said, holding out her hand. I kept staring at her glasses hanging from a chain around her neck.

"Wow, what a firm grip!" said Mrs. Garcia. "The kid's got a killer handshake," Mr. Tushman agreed.

She told me to call her Mrs. G. She pointed to photos on her bulletin board. Mom pointed to one of a baby and asked if it was hers. "No, my goodness! You've just made my day. He's my grandson."

"We're all going to take very good care of August," she said to Mom, and I saw her give Mom's hand a little squeeze. I looked at Mom's face, and that's when I realized she was just as nervous as I was. I guess I liked Mrs. Garcia — when she wasn't wearing her shiny smile.

✦ 两种微笑

Auggie 已经学会了分辨"真正的微笑"和"礼貌的微笑"。Mrs. Garcia 的"闪亮微笑"是后者——但她后来的真诚让他感到安心。

2
Jack, Julian 与 Charlotte
Jack Will, Julian, and Charlotte · "It'll be okay, August"
紧张初次见面

Mr. Tushman's office was full of cool stuff — a floating globe, a Rubik's cube made of mirrors, student artwork framed on the walls. I liked it there.

Then I heard voices outside: kids' voices. My heart literally started beating like I'd just run the longest race in the world.

The thing is, when I was little, I never minded meeting new kids because all the kids I met were really little, too. What's cool about really little kids is that they don't say stuff to try to hurt your feelings. But big kids: they know what they're saying.

One of the reasons I grew my hair long last year was that I like how my bangs cover my eyes: it helps me block out the things I don't want to see.

Mrs. Garcia knocked. "They're here, Mr. Tushman."

Two boys and a girl walked in. None of them looked over at me or Mom. "August, this is Jack Will," Mr. Tushman said. Jack Will looked at me and put out his hand. When I shook it, he kind of half smiled and said: "Hey," and looked down really fast.

"This is Julian." Julian did the same thing: took my hand, forced a smile, looked down fast. "And Charlotte." Charlotte had the blondest hair I've ever seen. She didn't shake my hand but gave me a quick little wave and smiled.

Mr. Tushman asked them to give me a tour. "So, is that okay with you, August?" I didn't answer. Then I saw Mom's face — she seemed more scared than I was. I just nodded.

✦ 大孩子的不同

小时候的童言无忌不会伤人，但大孩子"知道自己在说什么"——这是 Auggie 对外界最大的恐惧。他留长发挡眼睛，是保护自己的方式。

3
大参观
The Grand Tour · "Do you have any more questions?"
学校冷遇

We went up to the third floor. No one said a word. Julian opened the door to room 301 — our homeroom. He talked about Ms. Petosa, the teacher. Charlotte corrected him. "Not what I heard," answered Julian, "but whatever."

At the science lab, Julian stood in front of the half-opened door, talking without looking at me. Jack pushed past him: "Dude, let him go inside so he can check it out." When I walked over, Julian moved out of the way quickly, like he was afraid I might accidentally touch him.

Julian started pointing sarcastically: "That's the incubator. That big black thing is the chalkboard. These are the desks. Those are the Bunsen burners. This is chalk. This is the eraser."

"I'm sure he knows what an eraser is," Charlotte said, sounding a little like Via.

"How would I know what he knows?" Julian answered. "Mr. Tushman said he's never been to a school before."

I asked what "homeroom" meant. Charlotte explained kindly. "Do you have any more questions?" Jack asked. I asked if his name was Jack or Jack Will. "Jack is my first name. Will is my last name." Julian laughed: "Ha! You thought his name was Jackwill!" Jack shrugged: "Some people call me by my first and last name. I don't know why. Anyway, can we go now?"

✦ 细微的距离

Julian 躲闪的身体动作比任何话语都伤人。Charlotte 像姐姐 Via 一样纠正他。Jack 虽然话不多，但已经开始站在 Auggie 这边。

4
演出厅
The Performance Space · "Julian's a jerk"
对峙友情萌芽

Charlotte didn't stop talking as we headed to the performance space. She described the play Oliver! — she played Oliver even though she's a girl. Julian ran ahead, waving for me to follow.

While Charlotte talked, I noticed Julian staring at me out of the corner of his eye. This is something I see people do a lot with me. They think I don't know they're staring, but I can tell from the way their heads are tilted.

"Can I ask you a question?" Julian said. "What's the deal with your face? I mean, were you in a fire or something?"

"Julian, that's so rude!" said Charlotte.

"I'm not being rude, I'm just asking a question."

Charlotte said I was born like that. "I thought maybe he was in a fire, too," Julian insisted.

"Geez, Julian," said Jack. "Just shut up."

Jack held the doors open for me as we left the auditorium. He looked at me right in the face, kind of daring me to look back at him, which I did. Then I actually smiled. The thing is, my mouth doesn't go up at the corners the way other people's mouths do. It just goes straight across my face. But somehow Jack Will got that I had smiled at him. And he smiled back.

"Julian's a jerk," he whispered before the others reached us. "But, dude, you're gonna have to talk." I nodded. Then I looked up at Julian. "The word's 'supposedly,' by the way."

"What are you talking about?" "You said 'supposably' before." Julian insisted he didn't. But I could tell from the way he looked at me that he wasn't really sorry at all.

✦ 真正的连接

当 Auggie 对 Julian 纠正语法时，他展现了自己的智慧和勇气。Jack 读懂了他独特的微笑——这是全书第一个真正的友谊时刻。
