1
永泽的道歉
Nagasawa's Apology
对话 人物

There was no sign of Midori at the next day's lecture, either. What was happening with her? Ten days had gone by since I last talked to her on the telephone. I thought about giving her a call, but decided against it. She had said that she would call me.

第二天的课上依然没有绿子的身影。她到底怎么了？上次通电话已经过了十天。我想过给她打电话，但最后还是没打。她说过她会打给我的。

That Thursday I saw Nagasawa in the dining hall. He sat down next to me with a tray full of food and apologized for having made our "party" so unpleasant.

那个周四我在食堂见到了永泽。他端着满满一托盘饭菜在我旁边坐下，为上次"聚会"搞得不愉快而道歉。

"Never mind," I said. "I should be thanking you for a great dinner. I have to admit, though, it was a funny way to celebrate your first job."

"没关系，"我说，"我还得感谢你请我吃了顿好饭。不过我得承认，这庆祝你找到第一份工作的方式可真够特别的。"

"What's with all the apologizing?" I asked. "Are you sick?" "I may be," he said with a few little nods. "Hatsumi tells me you told her to leave me."

"你今天怎么一个劲儿道歉？"我问，"生病了？""也许是吧，"他轻轻点了点头，"初美说，你劝她离开我。"

"I made up with Hatsumi," he said. "I'm not surprised." "She's a great girl," I said, slurping my miso soup. "I know," he said with a sigh. "A little too great for me."

"我和初美和好了。""不出所料。""她是个好女孩，"我边喝味噌汤边说。"我知道，"他叹了口气，"对我来说太好了点儿。"

✦ 永泽的自我认知

永泽难得地表现出脆弱的一面。他知道初美太好，好得配不上她——但他永远无法改变自己。

2
绿子旅行归来
Midori Returns from Her Trip
重逢 对话

I was sleeping the sleep of death when the buzzer rang to let me know I had a call. It brought me back from the absolute core of sleep in total confusion. The clock said 6:15, but I had no idea if that meant A.M. or P.M.

我正睡得死去活来的时候，传呼铃响了。它把我从睡眠的最深处彻底拽了出来，我完全搞不清状况。时钟显示六点十五分，但我分不清是早上还是晚上。

"Hey, Watanabe, are you free now?" Midori asked. "I dunno, what's today?" "Friday." "Morning or evening?" "Evening, of course! You're so weird! Let's see, it's, uh, six-eighteen p.m."

"喂，渡边，你现在有空吗？""我不知道，今天星期几？""星期五。""上午还是晚上？""当然是晚上啦！你真怪！现在是下午六点十八分。"

When I got to Dug, Midori was sitting at the far end of the counter with a drink. She wore a man's wrinkled white balmacaan coat, a thin yellow sweater, blue jeans, and two bracelets on one wrist.

我到Dug的时候，绿子坐在吧台尽头，面前放着一杯酒。她穿着一件皱巴巴的白色男式巴尔玛肯大衣、一件薄薄的黄色毛衣、蓝色牛仔裤，一只手腕上戴着两只手镯。

"I took a trip," she said. "Just got back." "South to Nara and north to Aomori." "On the same trip?!" "Don't be stupid. I may be strange, but I can't go north and south at the same time. I went to Nara with my boyfriend, and then I took off to Aomori alone."

"我出去旅行了，刚回来。""去哪儿了？""南到奈良，北到青森。""同一趟？！""别傻了。我再怪也不能同时往北又往南。我和男朋友去了奈良，然后一个人去了青森。"

"We got plastered and went to bed—both of us out cold. We slept for hours, and if the phone rang or something, we just let it go. Dead to the world. Finally, after we woke up, we ordered sushi and talked about what to do. We decided to close the shop for a while and enjoy ourselves."

"我们喝得烂醉去睡觉——两人都不省人事。睡了很久，电话响了也不接。睡得跟死了一样。后来醒了叫了寿司，商量以后怎么办。决定关了店门，好好享受一阵子。"

"You have no idea how great it felt!"

"你不知道那有多痛快！"

✦ 葬礼后的解放

父亲的葬礼之后，绿子和姐姐终于从漫长的照料中解脱出来。她语气轻松，但背后是"连眼泪都流干了"的疲劳。

3
卫生棉条的故事
The Tampon Story
幽默 绿子

"The second we walked into the hotel room and dumped our bags, my period started. A real gusher." I couldn't help laughing. "Hey, it's not funny. I was a week early! I couldn't stop crying when that happened."

"我们一进酒店房间放下行李，月经就来了。量大得跟喷泉似的。"我忍不住笑了。"喂，这不好笑。提前了一个礼拜！我当时哭得停不下来。"

"Things haven't been right between me and my boyfriend ever since the tampon incident." "Tampon incident?"

"自从那次卫生棉条事件之后，我和男朋友的关系就一直不对劲。""卫生棉条事件？"

"Yeah, I was out drinking with him and a few of his friends about a month ago and I told them the story of a woman in my neighborhood who blew out a tampon when she sneezed. Funny, right?" "That is funny," I said with a laugh. "Yeah, all the other guys thought so, too. But he got mad and said I shouldn't be talking about such dirty things. Such a wet blanket!"

"嗯，一个月前我和他还有他几个朋友喝酒，讲了附近一个女人打喷嚏把卫生棉条喷出来的故事。挺好笑吧？""是挺好笑的，"我笑了。"是啊，别人也都觉得好笑。可他生气了，说我不该说这种下流话。真扫兴！"

"He's a wonderful guy, but he can be really narrow-minded when it comes to stuff like that," said Midori. "Like, he gets mad if I wear anything but white underwear. Don't you think that's narrow-minded?"

"他是个好人，可对这种事儿特别小气，"绿子说。"比如我只要不穿白色内衣他就生气。你说这不算小气吗？"

✦ 绿子的坦诚与男友的拘谨

绿子天然的坦诚与男友的保守形成鲜明对比。卫生棉条事件看似滑稽，实则是两人关系裂痕的缩影。

4
情色电影
The S&M Movie
喜剧 场景

"Hey, why don't we go now and see a dirty movie?" Midori suggested. "A real, filthy S and M one."

"喂，我们现在去看色情电影吧？"绿子提议，"真正的、肮脏的SM片。"

We went from the bar to an eel shop, and from there to one of Shinjuku's most run-down theaters for an adult triple feature. Inside, the theater had some kind of undefinable smell.

我们从酒吧去了鳗鱼店，然后去了新宿最破旧的电影院之一看成人三片连映。里面有种说不出的气味。

Midori's eyes were glued to the screen. I was impressed: anyone watching a movie with such fierce intensity was getting more than her money's worth. She kept reporting her thoughts to me: "Omigod, will you look at that," or "Three guys at once! They're going to tear her open!" or "I'd like to try that on somebody, Watanabe."

绿子目不转睛地盯着屏幕。我暗暗佩服：一个人看电影看得这么投入，绝对值回票价了。她不停地向我汇报感想——"天哪你看那个"、"三个男人同时上！把她撕开了！"、"我真想拿谁试试这招，渡边。"

"That was fun," said Midori. "Let's try it again sometime." "They just keep doing the same things," I said. "Well, what else can they do? We all just keep doing the same things."

"真有意思，"绿子说，"下次再来看。""他们翻来覆去就那些东西。""那还能做什么呢？我们不都一样吗？翻来覆去就那些事。"

✦ 绿式的哲学

绿子的率真在这个场景中达到极致——她对SM电影的评论、对黑暗影院的蔑视，都展现出她无所顾忌、直面一切的生命力。

绿子家过夜
Staying at Midori's
夜晚 亲密

"But I'm so lonely! I want to be with someone! I know I'm doing terrible things to you, making demands and not giving you anything in return, saying whatever pops into my head, dragging you out of your room and forcing you to take me everywhere, but you're the only one I can do stuff like that to!"

"可我太寂寞了！我想跟人待在一起！我知道我对你很过分，只管提要求却什么都不回报你，想到什么说什么，把你从房间里拉出来到处拽着我跑，但你是我唯一可以这样对待的人！"

"I have never been able to have my own way with anybody, not once in the twenty years I've been alive."

"我活了二十年，从来没有跟任何人任性过一次。"

"And now I'm really really really tired and I want to fall asleep listening to someone tell me how much they like me and how pretty I am and stuff. That's all I want."

"而现在我真的真的真的累了，我想听着别人说喜欢我、说我有多漂亮之类的话入睡。就这些。"

We took the Yamanote Line to Otsuka, and soon we were raising the metal shutter that sealed off the front of the Kobayashi Bookstore. A paper sign on the shutter read "Temporarily Closed." The smell of old paper filled the dark shop, as if the shutter had not been opened for a long time.

我们坐山手线到大塚，然后抬起小林书店的卷帘门。卷帘门上贴着"临时停业"的纸条。黑暗的店里充满旧纸的气息，仿佛卷帘门很久没有打开过了。

Half the shelves were empty, and most of the magazines had been tied in bundles for returns. That hollow, chilly feeling I had experienced on my first visit had only deepened. The place looked like a hulk abandoned on the shore.

一半书架已经空了，大部分杂志都捆好了准备退货。我第一次来时感受到的那种空洞、冰冷的感觉更深了。这地方看上去像搁浅在海滩上的残骸。

✦ 绿子唯一的任性

"我这辈子从来没跟任何人任性过"——绿子这句话道出了她强悍外表下的极度压抑。她只有在渡边面前才能放下一切。

春天的熊
The Spring Bear
经典 爱情

Practically falling over the edge of Midori's little bed, I held her in my arms. Nose against my chest, Midori set her hands on my hips.

我几乎要从绿子那张小床的边缘掉下去，但还是抱住了她。她把鼻子抵在我胸前，双手放在我的腰上。

"C'mon, say something to me," Midori said with her face buried in my chest. "Whaddya want me to say?" "Anything. Something to make me feel good."

"来，跟我说点什么，"绿子把脸埋在我胸口说。"你想让我说什么？""什么都行。让我开心的。"

"You're really cute, Midori," I corrected myself. "Whaddya mean really cute?" "So cute the mountains crumble and the oceans dry up."

"绿子，你真可爱。""什么叫真可爱？""可爱到山崩地裂、海枯石烂。"

"How much is a lot?" "Like a spring bear," I said. "A spring bear?" Midori looked up again. "What's that all about? A spring bear."

"有多少？""就像春天的熊一样。""春天的熊？"绿子又抬起头，"春天的熊是怎么回事？"

"You're walking through a field all by yourself one day in spring, and this sweet little bear cub with velvet fur and shiny little eyes comes walking along. And he says to you, 'Hi, there, little lady. Want to tumble with me?' So you and the bear cub spend the whole day in each other's arms, tumbling down this clover-covered hill. Nice, huh?"

"春天原野里，你一个人正走着，对面走来一只可爱的小熊，毛茸茸的像天鹅绒，眼睛圆鼓鼓的。它这么对你说道：'你好，小姐，和我一块儿打滚玩好么？'接着，你就和小熊抱在一起，顺着长满三叶草的山坡咕噜咕噜滚下去，整整玩了一天。你说棒不棒？"

"That's how much I like you." "That is the best thing I've ever heard," said Midori, cuddling up against my chest.

"我就这么喜欢你。""这是我这辈子听到的最好的话了，"绿子说着，依偎在我胸口。

✦ 全书最经典的表白

"春天的熊"——村上最著名的比喻之一。以纯粹的童真表达最深切的爱情，是绿子与渡边之间最柔软的时刻。

父亲的遗照前
Before Father's Portrait
奇想 绿子

"Know what I did the other day?" Midori asked. "I got all naked in front of my father's picture. Took off every stitch of clothing and let him have a good, long look. Kind of in a yoga position. Like, 'Here, Daddy, these are my tits, and this is my cunt.'"

"你知道我前几天做了什么吗？"绿子问道。"我在我爸的遗照前脱得精光。一件不留，让他好好看了个遍。有点像瑜伽姿势。就是说——'爸，你看，这是你女儿的奶子，这是你女儿的屄。' "

"Why in the hell would you do something like that?" I asked. "I don't know, I just wanted to show him. I mean, half of me comes from his sperm, right? Why shouldn't I show him? 'Here's the daughter you made.' I was a little drunk at the time."

"你为什么要做那种事？""不知道，我只是想让他看看。我身体的一半来自他的精子，不是吗？给他看看有什么不好？'这是你造出来的女儿。'我那时喝了点酒。"

"My sister walked in and fell over. There I was in front of my father's memorial portrait all naked with my legs spread. I guess anybody'd be kinda surprised."

"我姐走进来吓了一跳。我就那么裸体叉开腿坐在父亲遗像前。我想谁看到都会吓一跳吧。"

"Tell me, Watanabe, what did you think of my father?" "I'm not good with people I've just met, but it didn't bother me being alone with him. I felt pretty comfortable with him. We talked about all kinds of stuff." "What kind of stuff?" "Euripides," I said.

"说真的，渡边，你觉得我父亲怎么样？""我不太擅长跟初次见面的人打交道，但跟他单独在一起倒没觉得别扭。挺自在的。聊了很多。""聊什么？""欧里庇得斯。"我说。

✦ 赤裸的诚实

绿子在父亲遗照前脱光衣服——这个惊世骇俗的举动恰恰是她最纯粹的诚实：以自己的存在本身面对死亡，用身体宣告"我是你造出来的"。

8
读《在轮下》
Reading Beneath the Wheel
书籍 孤独

I sat at the kitchen table, drinking my beer and reading Beneath the Wheel. I had first read the novel the year I entered middle school. And now, eight years later, here I was, reading the same book in a girl's kitchen, wearing the undersized pajamas of her dead father. Funny.

我坐在厨房桌边喝着啤酒读《在轮下》。第一次读这小说是初中入学那年。八年后，我在一个女孩的厨房里读同一本书，穿着她死去的父亲那件偏小的睡衣。真有意思。

The book did have its dated moments, but as a novel it wasn't bad. I moved through it slowly, enjoying it line by line, in the hushed bookstore in the middle of the night. A dusty bottle of brandy stood on a shelf in the kitchen. I poured a little into a coffee cup and sipped it.

这本书虽然有些过时的地方，但作为小说并不差。我慢慢读着，一行一行地品味，在深夜寂静的书店里。厨房架子上有瓶落满灰尘的白兰地。我倒了一点在咖啡杯里，小口啜饮。

I went to check on Midori a little before three, but she was sound asleep. She must have been exhausted. The lights from the block of shops beyond the window cast a soft white glow, like moonlight, over the room. Midori slept with her back to the light. She lay so perfectly still, she might have been frozen stiff. Bending over, I caught the sound of her breathing. She slept just like her father.

快三点时我去看了看绿子，她睡得很沉。一定是累坏了。窗外街区的灯光投进房间，像月光一样柔和的白光。绿子背对着光睡着，一动不动，仿佛冻僵了。我俯身听她的呼吸。她睡得跟她父亲一模一样。

By the time I finished the book, the sky was growing light. I made myself some instant coffee and used some notepaper and a ballpoint pen I found on the table to write a message to Midori: "I drank some of your brandy. I bought a copy of Beneath the Wheel. It got light out, so I'm going home. Good-bye." Then, after some hesitation, I wrote, "You look really cute when you're sleeping."

书读完的时候，天已经亮了。我冲了杯速溶咖啡，用桌上的便签纸和圆珠笔给绿子留了张条："我喝了你的白兰地。买了一本《在轮下》。天亮了，我回去了。再见。"犹豫了一下，又加上一句："你睡着的样子真可爱。"

✦ 深夜的静谧

渡边在凌晨的厨房里读黑塞的小说，穿着死者睡衣——这个画面完美地呈现了小说的基调：生者停留在死者的空间中，静默地等待黎明。

直子的来信
Naoko's Letters
书信 直子

I wrote to Naoko every week, and she often wrote back. None of her letters was very long. Soon there were mentions of the cold November mornings and evenings.

我每周给直子写信，她也常常回信。她的信都不长。不久信里开始提到十一月早晚的寒冷。

"You went back to Tokyo just about the time the fall weather was deepening, so for a time I couldn't tell whether the hole that opened up inside me was from missing you or from the change of season. Reiko and I talk about you all the time."

"你回东京的时候秋意正浓，所以有段时间我说不清心底那个洞是因为想念你，还是因为季节转换。玲子和我不停地聊你。"

"When I'm lonely at night, people talk to me from the darkness. They talk to me the way trees moan in the wind at night. Kizuki; my sister: they talk to me like that all the time. They're lonely too, and looking for someone to talk to."

"夜里孤独的时候，有人从黑暗中对我说活。就像夜里树木在风中呻吟那样对我说话。木月，我姐姐——他们总是那样对我说话。他们也很孤独，在找人说活。"

"Speaking of Midori, she sounds like an interesting person. Reading your letter, I got the feeling she might be in love with you. When I told that to Reiko, she said, 'Well, of course she is! Even I am in love with Watanabe!'"

"说起来，绿子听起来是个很有趣的人。读你的信时，我觉得她可能爱上你了。我把这想法告诉玲子，她说：'那当然啦！连我都爱上渡边了呢！'"

✦ 黑暗中的声音

直子的信透露了她内心最深的黑暗：孤独的夜里"有人从黑暗中对她说话"。木月和姐姐的亡魂从未离去。

10
生日毛衣
The Birthday Sweater
礼物 温暖

Three days after my twentieth birthday, a package arrived for me from Naoko. Inside I found a wine-colored crewneck sweater and a letter.

二十岁生日过后第三天，直子的包裹寄到了。里面是一件酒红色的圆领毛衣和一封信。

"Happy Birthday! I hope you have a happy year being twenty. My own year of being twenty looks as if it's going to end with me as miserable as ever, but I'd really like it if you could have your share of happiness and mine combined. Really."

"生日快乐！希望你的二十岁是幸福的一年。我自己的二十岁，看来就要像现在这样凄凄惨惨地结束了，但我真的希望你能把你那份幸福和我的那份合在一起。真的。"

"Reiko and I each knit half of this sweater. If I had done it all by myself, it would have taken until next Valentine's Day. The good half is Reiko's, and the bad half is mine. Reiko is so good at everything she does, I sometimes hate myself when I'm watching her. I mean, I haven't got one single thing I'm really good at!"

"玲子和我各织了一半。要是全由我一个人织，恐怕要到明年情人节才能织完。织得好的一半是玲子的，织得差的一半是我的。玲子做什么都那么好，有时候看着她我都恨自己。真的，我一件拿手的事都没有！"

The package had a short note from Reiko, too. "How are you? For you, Naoko may be the pinnacle of happiness, but for me she's just a clumsy girl. Still, we managed to finish this sweater in time for your birthday. Handsome, isn't it? We picked the color and the style. Happy Birthday."

包裹里还有玲子的一张便条："你好吗？对你来说，直子也许是幸福的巅峰，可对我来说她就是个笨手笨脚的姑娘。不过我们还是赶在你生日前织完了这件毛衣。挺漂亮的吧？颜色和款式是我们挑的。生日快乐。"

✦ 两个人的礼物

直子和玲子各织了一半的毛衣——"织得好的一半是玲子的，织得差的一半是我的。"直子永远在拿自己跟别人比较，永远觉得自己不够好。
