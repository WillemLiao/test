R. J. Palacio
Jack 和 Auggie 的友谊日渐深厚——他们一起在课堂上偷着乐，一起编造"粘液枪"的幻想。Auggie 的十岁生日派对来了，但大部分同学都没有来。十月到了，万圣节即将来临。
1
Jack Will
Jack Will · "Dude, you should sue your doctor!"
友谊幽默

I hung out with Jack in homeroom, English, history, computer, music, and science — every class we had together. The teachers assigned seats, and I ended up next to Jack in every single one. Either the teachers were told to put us together, or it was a totally incredible coincidence.

One time a huge eighth grader knocked me down on the stairs. When he helped me up and saw my face, he just said: "Whoa!" For some reason, me and Jack started cracking up. "That guy made the funniest face!"

Jack whispered: "Do you ever want to beat those kids up? I think you should get a secret squirt gun attached to your eyes. Every time someone stares at you, squirt them in the face." "With some green slime." "No: with slug juice mixed with dog pee."

Then Jack asked: "Are you always going to look this way? Can't you get plastic surgery or something?" I smiled: "Hello? This is after plastic surgery!" Jack clapped his hand over his forehead. "Dude, you should sue your doctor!"

We laughed so hard Mr. Roche made us switch chairs with the kids next to us.

✦ 真正的友谊

Jack 是唯一一个能拿 Auggie 的脸开玩笑的人——这恰恰说明他不把它当回事。能一起笑出来的,才是真朋友。

2
十月的格言
Mr. Browne's October Precept · "Your deeds are your monuments"
格言思考

Mr. Browne's precept for October was: YOUR DEEDS ARE YOUR MONUMENTS.

He told us this was written on an Egyptian guy's tombstone from thousands of years ago. Our homework was to write a paragraph about what it meant.

This is what I wrote: "This precept means that we should be remembered for the things we do. The things we do are the most important things of all. They are more important than what we say or what we look like. … Your deeds are like your monuments. Built with memories instead of with stone."

✦ 行为的丰碑

一个十岁孩子写下了全书最深刻的感悟：我们的外表不重要,我们的行为才是留给世界的纪念碑。

3
苹果
Apples · "The apple doesn't fall far from the tree"
生日冷落

My birthday is October 10. I like my birthday: 10/10. I asked Mom if I could have a big bowling party and invite everyone in my homeroom plus Summer. "I have to invite everyone because I don't want anyone to get their feelings hurt."

A couple of weeks later, I asked who was coming. "Jack Will, Summer. Reid Kingsley. Both Maxes." "That's it? That's like five people."

Mom said a lot of people had plans. "What did Julian give as his reason?" "His mom was the only person who didn't RSVP at all. I guess the apple doesn't fall far from the tree."

The party was small but great. Jack, Summer, Reid, Tristan, both Maxes, and Christopher came from Bridgeport. All the grown-ups bowled in the lane next to ours. It felt like a lot of people were there.

✦ 五个人就够了

全班 20+ 人,只有 5 个同学来了。但 Auggie 说"派对很棒"——因为他学会了看杯子里还有多少水,而不是空了多少。

4
万圣节
Halloween · Boba Fett vs. Unicorn
万圣节Summer

At lunch Summer asked what I was going to be for Halloween. "Boba Fett." She wanted to be a unicorn but thought it was too dorky. "Who cares what other people think, right?" I said.

Summer hardly ever sees me during the day — the only class we have together is English. But ever since that first lunch, we've sat at the summer table together every day, just the two of us.

"You know, that's what I like best about you. I feel like I can tell you anything."

I gave her a thumbs-up. "Cool beans."

✦ 夏天和独角兽

Summer 想扮成独角兽却不敢,Auggie 说"管别人怎么想"——这两个被主流排斥的孩子,在午餐桌上找到了最安全的港湾。
