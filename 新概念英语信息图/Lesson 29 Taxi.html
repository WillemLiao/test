02原文故事

Captain F has bought an unusual taxi and has begun a new service.

The taxi is a small Swiss aeroplane called a P.

This wonderful plane can carry seven passengers.

The most surprising thing about it, however, is that it can land anywhere: on snow, water, or even on a ploughed field.

Captain F first passenger was a doctor who flew from Birmingham to a lonely village in the Welsh mountains.

Since then, Captain F has flown passengers to many unusual places.

Once he landed on the roof of a block of flats and on another occasion, he landed in a deserted car park.

Captain F has just refused a strange request from a businessman.

The man wanted to fly to R, a lonely island in the Atlantic Ocean,

but Captain F did not take him because the trip was too dangerous.

本・弗西特机长买了一辆不同寻常的出租汽车，并开始了一项新的业务。

这辆“出租汽车”是一架小型瑞士飞机，叫“皮勒特斯・波特“号。

然而，最令人惊奇的是它能够在任何地方降落：雪地上，水面上，甚至刚耕过的田里。

弗西特机长的第一名乘客是位医生，他从伯明翰飞往威尔士山区一个偏僻的村庄。

从那时开始，弗西特机长已经载送乘客到过许多不寻常的地方。

一次，他把飞机降落在了一栋公寓楼的屋顶上；还有一次，降落在了一个废弃的停车场上。
