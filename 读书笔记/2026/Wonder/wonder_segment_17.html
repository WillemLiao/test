停学在家的 Jack 终于向妈妈坦白了万圣节的真相。一封封电子邮件揭示了这场风波的背后:Julian 的妈妈试图将 August 赶出学校,Tushman 先生用一封优雅而坚定的信回击。而 Jack 和 August 之间的短信——"ok"——只用两个字就修复了破碎的友谊。但回到学校,迎接 Jack 的是 Julian 精心策划的冷暴力。

巧合的圣诞卡
Weirdly Enough · "People aren't always so great"
坦白母子

When Jack got home from suspension, the mail held holiday cards from both Julian's and August's families. Julian's was a picture of him in a tie. August's featured a cute old dog with reindeer antlers: "Ho-Ho-Ho! … Peace on Earth. Love, Nate, Isabel, Olivia, August (and Daisy)."

Jack's mom looked at August's card carefully. "We're very lucky, Jack. There's so much we take for granted. …" "I know."

Jack told her about Julian's mom Photoshopping August out of the class picture. "That's just awful," said Mom. "People are just … they're not always so great."

Then Jack finally told her everything: why he punched Julian, that August was his ex-friend now, and the whole truth about Halloween. "Is that why you hit Julian?" "No." And then I told her why. And I told her about Halloween.

信件与短信
The Letters · "ok" · "yes dude we'r frenz agen"
正直和解

Jack wrote to Mr. Tushman: "I am very, very sorry for punching Julian. … I would really rather not tell you why I did what I did because it doesn't really make it right anyway."

Mr. Tushman replied: "One thing I've learned after being a middle-school director for twenty years: there are almost always more than two sides to every story. … While nothing justifies striking another student—ever—I also know good friends are sometimes worth defending. Keep up the good work, and keep being the fine boy we all know you are."

Then came Julian's mother's email — attacking August's admission to Beecher Prep, calling him a burden. Mr. Tushman's response was masterful: "August does not have special needs. He is neither disabled, handicapped, nor developmentally delayed in any way. … I did not think asking these children to be especially kind to a new student would place any extra 'burdens or hardships' on them. In fact, I thought it would teach them a thing or two about empathy, and friendship, and loyalty. As it turns out, Jack Will didn't need to learn any of these virtues—he already had them in abundance."

Jack sent a Facebook friend request. He left a message: "Hey august. Its me Jack Will. I noticed im not on ur friends list anymore. Hope u friend me agen cuz im really sorry. I know why ur mad at me now Im sorry I didn't mean the stuff I said. I was so stupid. I hope u can 4give me."

The text messages flew back and forth. August's first reply: "got ur message u know why im mad at u now?? did Summer tell u?" Jack told him about "Bleeding Scream." August asked: "whyd u punch him????????" Jack: "I dunno" August: "liar. I bet he said something about me right?"

Then August asked the question that had been haunting him: "wud u really wan to kill urself if u wer me???" Jack's answer was immediate: "no!!!!! I swear on my life. but dude- I would want 2 kill myself if I were Julian ;)"

August: "lol. yes dude we'r frenz agen."

✦ 朋友了

整本书最动人的和解——没有长篇大论,没有拥抱。"ok"和"lol"就足够了。两个五年级的孩子用最笨拙的拼写修复了友谊。而 Tushman 的信和 Melissa 的邮件之间,展现了成年世界的善与恶各是什么样的。

"Despite what Tushman said, there was no 'clean slate' when I went back to school in January. In fact, things were totally weird from the second I got to my locker."

Amos barely nodded. Henry looked away. Tristan looked nervous, like he was afraid of Jack. "I've got a form of the Plague now, is what I thought. This is Julian's payback."

At lunch, Jack sat with Luca and Isaiah — middle-of-the-road kids. They got their food and never came back. He found a table way on the other end of the cafeteria — near Julian. "They weren't at Julian's table, but they were near him, like on the fringe of popularity."

Jack sat alone. "It felt really awful being at the table by myself. I felt like everyone was watching me. It also made me feel like I had no friends. I decided to skip lunch and go read in the library."
