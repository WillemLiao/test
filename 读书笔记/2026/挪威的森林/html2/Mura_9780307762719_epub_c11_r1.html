1
直子死后
After Naoko's Death
死亡 哀悼

REIKO WROTE TO ME SEVERAL TIMES AFTER NAOKO'S DEATH. IT was not my fault, she said. It was nobody's fault, any more than you could blame someone for the rain. But I never answered her. What could I have said? What good would it have done? Naoko no longer existed in this world; she had become a fistful of ash.

直子死后，玲子给我写了好几封信。她说不是我的错。谁的错都不是，就像不能怪罪下雨一样。但我一封也没回。我能说什么呢？说了又有什么用呢？直子已经不在这个世界上了，变成了一捧灰。

They held a quiet funeral for Naoko in Kobe at the end of August, and when it was over, I went back to Tokyo. I told my landlord I would be away for a while and my boss at the Italian restaurant that I wouldn't be coming in to work. To Midori I wrote a short note: I couldn't say anything just yet, but I hoped she would wait for me a little longer.

八月底，在神户为直子举行了一场安静的葬礼。结束后我回到东京。告诉房东我要离开一段时间，跟意大利餐厅的老板说我不来上班了。给绿子写了一封短信：我还什么都不能说，但希望她再等我一段时间。

I spent the next three days in movie theaters, and after I had seen every new movie in Tokyo, I packed my knapsack, took all my money out of the bank, went to Shinjuku Station, and took the first express train I could find heading out of town.

接下来的三天我泡在电影院里，看完东京所有新上映的电影之后，我收拾好背包，取出银行里所有的钱，去了新宿站，跳上第一列开往远方的特快列车。

✦ 一捧灰

"直子已不在这个世界上，变成了一捧灰。"——没有比这更彻底的终结。渡边用流浪来逃避这个事实。

2
流浪
Wandering
旅行 孤独

Where I went in my travels, it's impossible for me to recall. I remember the sights and sounds and smells clearly enough, but the names of the towns are gone.

我旅行去了哪里，已经记不清了。景色、声音、气味都清清楚楚地记得，但城镇的名字全都消失了。

I would move from town to town by train or bus or hitching a ride in a truck, spreading my sleeping bag out in empty lots or stations or parks or on riverbanks or the seashore. I once got them to let me sleep in the corner of a neighborhood police station, and another time slept by the side of a graveyard.

我坐火车、巴士或搭卡车从一个城镇移动到另一个城镇，在空地、车站、公园、河岸或海边摊开睡袋。有一次警察允许我在派出所角落里睡了一晚，还有一次睡在墓地旁边。

All I wanted was to put myself to sleep in towns I didn't know.

我只想在不认识的城镇让自己入睡。

The image of her was still too vivid in my memory. I could still see her enclosing my penis in her mouth, her hair falling across my belly. I could still feel her warmth, her breath against me, and that helpless moment when I could do nothing but come.

她的形象在记忆中仍然太鲜活了。我仍能看到她含住我的阴茎，头发散落在我的腹部。我仍能感受到她的温暖、她的呼吸，以及那个除了射精什么都无法做的无助时刻。

I felt sure that Naoko was still beside me, that I could just reach out and touch her. But no, she was not there; her flesh no longer existed in this world.

我确信直子还在我身边，只要伸手就能触到她。但不对，她不在那里；她的肉体已不在这个世界上。

✦ 无目的的漂流

渡边开始了没有目的的旅行。"只想在不认识的城镇里入睡"——睡眠成为对存在的唯一要求。

3
海边的渔夫
The Fisherman on the Beach
相遇 人性

ONE WINDY EVENING, as I lay wrapped in my sleeping bag, weeping, by the side of an abandoned hulk, a young fisherman happened by and offered me a cigarette. I accepted it and had my first smoke in over a year.

一个起风的傍晚，我裹在睡袋里躺在废弃船壳旁哭泣时，一个年轻的渔夫路过，递给我一支烟。我接过来——一年多来第一次抽烟。

He asked why I was crying, and almost by reflex I told him that my mother had died. I couldn't take the sadness, I said, and so I was on the road. He expressed his deep sympathy and brought a big bottle of sake and two glasses from his house.

他问我为什么哭，我几乎是下意识地说母亲去世了。太悲伤了，所以出来流浪。他表示了深切的同情，从家里拿来一大瓶清酒和两个杯子。

Who gives a damn about your mother? I lost Naoko! Her beautiful flesh has vanished from this world! Why the hell are you telling me about your goddamn mother?!

谁管你妈啊？我失去的是直子！她美丽的肉体已经从这个世界消失了！你他妈为什么跟我讲你妈的事？！

The young fisherman came back a half hour later with two boxes of sushi and a new bottle of sake... As he stood to go, he took a folded five-thousand-yen note from his pocket and shoved it into the pocket of my shirt. "Here," he said, "get yourself some healthy food. You look awful."

半小时后年轻渔夫回来了，带着两盒寿司和一瓶新酒……临走时他从口袋里掏出一张折好的五千日元钞票，塞进我衬衫口袋。"拿着，"他说，"买点有营养的东西。你看起来糟透了。"

"It's not money," he said, "it's my feelings. Don't think about it too much, just take it."

"这不是钱，"他说，"是我的心意。别想太多，收下吧。"

✦ 陌生人的善意

渔夫不知道渡边失去了谁，但他给出了力所能及的一切：酒、食物、钱。他的善意让渡边的谎言变得沉重——他醒悟到该回去了。

4
回到东京
Return to Tokyo
归途 思考

I had learned one thing from Kizuki's death, and I believed that I had made it a part of myself in the form of a philosophy: "Death is not the opposite of life but an innate part of life."

从木月之死中我学到了一件事，并且相信我已将它转化为自身哲学的一部分："死不是生的对立面，而是生的一部分。"

By living our lives, we nurture death. True as this might be, it was only one of the truths we had to learn. What I learned from Naoko's death was this: no truth can cure the sorrow we feel from losing a loved one. No truth, no sincerity, no strength, no kindness can cure that sorrow. All we can do is see it through to the end and learn something from it, but what we learn will be no help in facing the next sorrow that comes to us without warning.

活着，我们就在孕育死亡。这固然是真理，但只是我们必须学习的真理之一。从直子之死中我学到的是：没有任何真理能治愈失去所爱之人的悲伤。没有真理，没有真诚，没有力量，没有善意能治愈那种悲伤。我们所能做的只有坚持到底，从中学习些什么，但学到的东西对下一次突如其来的悲伤毫无帮助。

The month of traveling neither lifted my spirits nor softened the blow of Naoko's death. I arrived back in Tokyo in pretty much the same state in which I had left.

一个月的旅行既没有让我振作，也没有减轻直子之死的打击。我回到东京时，状态和离开时几乎一样。

I was overcome with a sense of my own defilement. Though I returned to Tokyo I did nothing for days but shut myself up in my room. My memory remained fixed on the dead rather than the living.

我被一种自我玷污的感觉笼罩。虽然回到了东京，但一连几天什么都没做，只是把自己关在房间里。我的记忆停留在死者身上，而不是生者身上。

✦ 新的领悟

"没有任何真理能治愈失去所爱之人的悲伤。"——直子之死教会渡边的东西比木月之死更深刻、更残酷。

5
玲子的来访
Reiko's Visit
玲子 重逢

"Do you mind if I come visit you the day after tomorrow?" "Visit me? You mean here in Tokyo?" "That's exactly what I mean. I want to have a good long talk with you."

"后天我去看你，行吗？""来看我？你是说来东京？""正是这个意思。我想和你好好聊聊。"

"You won't have any trouble recognizing me. I'm the old lady with the guitar case. There aren't many of those."

"你肯定能认出我。就是那个带着吉他箱的老太婆。这年头带吉他箱的老太婆不多。"

AND IN FACT, I had no trouble finding Reiko in the crowd. She wore a man's tweed jacket, white slacks, and red sneakers. Her hair was as short as ever, with the usual clumps sticking up. In her right hand she held a brown leather suitcase, and in her left hand a black guitar case. She gave me a big, wrinkly smile the moment she spotted me, and I found myself smiling back.

事实上，我在人群中一眼就认出了玲子。她穿着男式粗花呢夹克、白色长裤和红色运动鞋。头发和以前一样短，还是那几撮翘起来的。右手拎着棕色皮箱，左手提着黑色吉他箱。她一看见我就露出满脸皱纹的笑容，我也不由得笑了出来。

"I'm scared," she said. "So scared, I could go crazy just like that. I don't know what I'm supposed to do, flung out here all by myself." She paused. "But 'Go crazy just like that.' Kind of a cool expression, don't you think?"

"我害怕，"她说，"怕得差点就这样疯掉。被扔到外面来，一个人，我不知道该怎么办。"她停顿了一下。"不过'差点就这样疯掉'——这个说法还挺酷的，你不觉得吗？"

I smiled and took her hand. "Don't worry," I said. "You're gonna be O.K. Your own strength got you this far."

我笑了，握住她的手。"别担心，"我说，"你会没事的。你自己的坚强带你走到了这一步。"

✦ 玲子归来

直子死后，玲子离开了生活八年的疗养院，来东京找渡边。两人共享着对直子的记忆——一个死去的纽带将他们联结在一起。

6
最后的夜晚
The Last Night
夜晚 对话

It was one of those early autumn afternoons when the light is sharp and clear, exactly as it had been a year earlier when I visited Naoko in Kyoto. The clouds were white and narrow as bones, the sky wide open and high. The fragrance of the breeze, the tone of the light, the presence of tiny flowers in the grass, the subtle reverberations that accompanied sounds: all these told me that autumn had come again, increasing the distance between me and the dead with each cycle of the seasons. Kizuki was still seventeen, and Naoko twenty-one: forever.

那是一个初秋的午后，光线锐利而清澈，和一年前我去京都看直子时一模一样。云朵又白又细，像骨头一样；天空开阔而高远。微风的香气、光线的色调、草丛中小花的存在、伴随声音的细微回响——这一切都在告诉我秋天又来了，每个季节轮回都在增加我和死者之间的距离。木月永远是十七岁，直子永远是二十一岁。

Reiko took a tin of rice crackers from her bag and said it was a present for me. "What were you talking about so long over there?" I asked, munching on a cracker. "You, of course," said Reiko, cradling the cat and rubbing her cheek against it.

玲子从包里拿出一罐仙贝，说是给我的礼物。"你在那儿聊了那么久，说什么了？"我边嚼仙嘴边问。"当然是你啦，"玲子抱着猫，用脸颊蹭着它说。

"Nice shirt, don't you think?" she asked. "It is," I said. In fact it was a good-looking shirt, with a handsome pattern. "It's Naoko's," said Reiko.

"这衬衫不错吧？"她问。"不错，"我说。确实是一件很好看的衬衫，图案很漂亮。"是直子的，"玲子说。

"Naoko didn't leave a will or anything—except where her clothes were concerned. She scribbled one line on a memo pad on her desk. 'Please give all my clothes to Reiko.' She was a funny one, don't you think? Why would she be concerned about her clothes of all things when she's getting ready to die?"

"直子什么都没留下——除了关于衣服的。她在桌上的备忘录上写了一行字：'请把我所有的衣服都送给玲子。'她真是个有趣的人，你不觉得吗？都要死了，为什么偏偏在意衣服？"

✦ 永远的年龄

"木月永远是十七岁，直子永远是二十一岁。"——死者不再变老，而生者背负着时间的重担继续前行。

7
直子最后的日子
Naoko's Final Days
死亡 回忆

"So she says, 'I don't know, I just feel like talking about it. I'll stop if you'd rather not hear it.'"

"她说：'我不知道，我就是想说说。你不想听我就不说了。'"

"So she went on with her story. 'When he went inside me, I couldn't believe how much it hurt. It was my first time, after all. I was so wet, he slipped right in, but still, my brain fogged over—it hurt so much. He put it in as far as he could, I thought, but then he lifted my legs and went in even farther. That sent chills all through my body, like I was soaking in ice water.'"

"于是她继续讲。'他进入我的时候，我疼得不敢相信。毕竟是第一次。我那么湿，他一下就滑进去了，可我还是疼得脑子发蒙。我以为他已经全进去了，可他抬起我的腿，又往里进了一些。那一瞬间全身像被冷水浸泡一样发抖。'"

"But he realized I was in pain, so he stopped moving, and still deep inside me, he started kissing me all over—my hair, my neck, my breasts—for a long, long time. Little by little, the warmth returned to my body, and then, very slowly, he started to move. Oh, Reiko, it was so wonderful! Now it felt as if my brain was just going to melt away."

"但他发现我疼，就停了下来，还深深地留在我体内，开始吻我全身——头发、脖子、乳房——吻了很久很久。温暖一点一点地回到我身体里，然后他极其缓慢地开始动。啊，玲子，太美妙了！我感觉脑子都要融化了。"

"But she said, 'No, Reiko, I knew it would never happen again. I knew this was something that would come to me once, and leave, and never come back. This would be a once-in-a-lifetime thing. I had never felt anything like it before, and I've never felt anything like it since. I've never felt that I wanted to do it again, and I've never grown wet like that again.'"

"但她说：'不，玲子，我知道那再也不会发生了。我知道那是只来一次、然后永远不再回来的东西。一生只有一次。以前从未有过那样的感觉，以后也再没有过。再也没有想要过，再也没有湿过。'"

"I just don't want anybody going inside me again. I just don't want to be violated like that again—by anybody."

"我再也不想让任何人进入我的身体。我再也不想被任何人那样侵犯了。"

✦ 直子的最后告白

直子对玲子说出了那晚性爱的细节——一生只有一次的美好，和此后永恒的封闭。说完这些，她在凌晨离开，走向树林深处。

8
音乐葬礼
The Funeral of Music
音乐 告别

WE TOOK THE five-minute walk along the riverbank to the local public bath and came home feeling somewhat refreshed. I opened the bottle of wine and we sat on the veranda drinking it.

我们沿着河堤走了五分钟去公共澡堂，回来时感觉清爽了些。我开了瓶葡萄酒，两人坐在门廊上喝。

"Say, Watanabe, could you bring out another glass?" "Sure," I said. "But what for?" "We're going to have our own funeral for Naoko, just the two of us. One that's not so sad."

"喂，渡边，能再拿个杯子来吗？""好，"我说，"做什么？""我们要给直子办个自己的葬礼，就我们两个。不那么悲伤的。"

"Now what I want you to do is lay down a match every time I play a song, just set them in a row. I'm going to play every song I can think of."

"我每弹一首歌，你就放一根火柴，排成一列。我要把能想到的歌全弹一遍。"

She played "Norwegian Wood," "Yesterday," "Michelle," and "Something." She sang and played "Here Comes the Sun," then played "The Fool on the Hill." I laid seven matches in a row. "Seven songs," said Reiko, sipping more wine and smoking another cigarette. "Those guys sure knew something about the sadness of life, and gentleness."

她弹了《挪威的森林》、《昨日》、《米歇尔》和《某事》。她边弹边唱了《太阳出来了》，然后弹了《山上的傻子》。我放了七根火柴排成一排。"七首了，"玲子抿了一口酒，又点了一支烟。"那几个人啊，真懂得人生的悲伤和温柔。"

She performed a few Bacharach songs next: "Close to You," "Raindrops Keep Falling on My Head," "Walk On By," plus Laura Nyro's "Wedding Bell Blues." "Twenty," I said. "I'm like a human jukebox," Reiko exclaimed.

她接着弹了几首巴卡拉克的歌：《靠近你》、《雨滴不停落在头上》、《走过我身边》，还有劳拉·尼罗的《婚礼钟声蓝调》。"二十首，"我说。"我简直像个人形点唱机，"玲子叫道。

When the wine was gone, we turned to whiskey. The wine in the glass in the garden I poured over the stone lantern and replaced it with whiskey. "How's our count going?" Reiko asked. "Forty-eight," I said. For our forty-ninth song, Reiko played "Eleanor Rigby," and the fiftieth was another performance of "Norwegian Wood."

酒喝完了，我们换成了威士忌。我把花园石灯笼上那杯酒倒掉，换上了威士忌。"几首了？"玲子问。"四十八首。"第四十九首，玲子弹了《艾莉诺·瑞格比》，第五十首，又弹了一遍《挪威的森林》。

✦ 五十首歌的告别

这是全书最动人的段落之一。玲子用五十首歌为直子举办了一场音乐葬礼。《挪威的森林》弹了两次——一次开始，一次结束。

9
与玲子的一夜
The Night with Reiko
亲密 治愈

"Here's one more for good measure," she said, and for her fifty-first piece she played her favorite Bach fugue. When she was through, she said in a voice just above a whisper, "How about doing it with me, Watanabe?" "Strange," I said. "I was thinking the same thing."

"再来一首收尾吧，"说着她弹了第五十一首——她最喜欢的巴赫赋格曲。弹完后她用近乎耳语的声音说："渡边，跟我做爱好吗？""奇怪，"我说，"我也在想同样的事。"

WE WENT INSIDE and closed the curtains. Then, in the darkened room, Reiko and I sought out each other's bodies as if it were the most natural thing in the world for us to do.

我们走进屋里，拉上窗帘。然后在昏暗的房间里，玲子和我寻找着彼此的身体，仿佛这是世上最自然的事情。

"I've lived a strange life," said Reiko, "but I never thought I'd have my panties removed for me by a man nineteen years my junior." "Would you rather take them off yourself?" "No, go ahead. But don't be too shocked at all my wrinkles." "I like your wrinkles."

"我过了一辈子奇怪的生活，"玲子说，"但从没想到会有一个比我小十九岁的男人帮我脱内裤。""要不你自己来？""不，你来吧。不过别被我的皱纹吓到。""我喜欢你的皱纹。"

Altogether, we joined our bodies four times that night. At the end each time, Reiko would lie in my arms trembling slightly, eyes closed, and release a long sigh.

那晚我们总共结合了四次。每次之后，玲子都会在我怀里微微颤抖着闭上眼睛，长长地叹一口气。

"I never have to do this again," said Reiko, "for the rest of my life. Oh, please, Watanabe, tell me it's true. Tell me I can relax now because I've done enough to last a lifetime."

"我再也不用做这个了，"玲子说，"这辈子都不用。啊，渡边，请告诉我这是真的。告诉我可以放心了，因为我已经做够了一辈子的量。"

✦ 与过去的告别

渡边和玲子的肉体结合并非情欲，而是两个失去直子的人用身体完成共同的哀悼。这是对死者最后的告别仪式。

10
告别
Farewell
结束 开始

I accompanied her to Ueno Station. She carried her guitar and I carried her suitcase. We sat on a platform bench waiting for the train to pull in.

我送她到上野站。她提着吉他，我拎着行李箱。我们坐在月台的长椅上等火车进站。

"You know, Watanabe, truth is, I'm scared, going to Asahikawa by myself. So be sure to write to me. Whenever I read your letters, I feel you're right there next to me." "If that's what you want, I'll write all the time."

"说实话，渡边，一个人去旭川我还是害怕。所以一定要给我写信。每次读你的信，我都觉得你就在我身边。""如果你希望，我会一直写。"

"Don't forget about me," she said. "I won't forget you," I said. "Ever." "We may never meet again, but no matter where I go, I'll always remember you and Naoko."

"别忘了我。""我不会忘记你。永远不会。""我们也许再也不会见面了，但无论我去哪里，我都会永远记得你和直子。"

I saw that she was crying. Before I knew it, I was kissing her. Others on the platform were staring at us, but I didn't care about such things anymore. We were alive, she and I. And all we had to think about was continuing to live.

我看到她在哭。不知不觉间，我吻了她。月台上其他人在看着我们，但我已经不在乎这些了。我们都还活着，她和我。我们只需要考虑继续活下去。

I TELEPHONED MIDORI. "I have to talk to you," I said. "I have a million things to talk to you about. A million things we have to talk about. All I want in this world is you. I want to see you and talk. I want the two of us to begin everything from the beginning."

我给绿子打了电话。"我必须跟你谈谈，"我说，"有太多太多话要对你说。有太多太多事要商量。我在这世上想要的只有你。我想见你，想和你说话。我想我们俩从头开始一切。"

Midori responded with a long, long silence—the silence of all the misty rain in the world falling on all the new-mown lawns of the world. Forehead pressed against the glass, I shut my eyes and waited. At last, Midori's quiet voice broke the silence: "Where are you now?"

绿子以长长的沉默回应——世间所有的蒙蒙细雨落在世间所有的新剪草坪上的那种沉默。我把额头抵在玻璃上，闭上眼睛等待。终于，绿子安静的声音打破了沉默："你现在在哪里？"

Where was I now? Gripping the receiver, I raised my head and turned to see what lay beyond the telephone booth. Where was I now? I had no idea. No idea at all. Where was this place? All that flashed into my eyes were the countless shapes of people walking by to nowhere. Again and again, I called out for Midori from the dead center of this place that was no place.

我现在在哪里？我握着话筒抬起头，转身去看电话亭外的世界。我现在在哪里？我完全不知道。完全不知道。这到底是什么地方？映入眼帘的只有无数走向虚无的行人的身影。我一遍又一遍地从这个没有地方的地方的中心向绿子呼喊着。

✦ 全书终章：你在哪里？

"你现在在哪里？"——绿子的最后一句是全书的终结，也是新的开始。渡边在"没有地方的地方"向绿子呼喊，小说在此戛然而止。
