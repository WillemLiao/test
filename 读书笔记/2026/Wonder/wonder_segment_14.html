1
八月的电话
So in August · "He's deformed"
第一反应诚实

"So in August my parents got this call from Mr. Tushman, the middle-school director. And my Mom said: 'Maybe he calls all the new students to welcome them,' and my dad said: 'That's a lot of kids he'd be calling.'"

Mom's side of the phone call was a series of pauses and "Ohhhs." Then she explained: Mr. Tushman wanted Jack to be a "welcome buddy" for a boy who'd never been to school before.

"But why me?" "I told you. Your teachers told Mr. Tushman that you were the kind of kid who's known for being a good egg."

"What is it, sad?" Jack asked. His mom had said it was flattering but sad. "Well, apparently this boy has some sort of … um, I guess there's something wrong with his face … or something like that."

"Do I have to?" Jack asked. "Well, no, of course not," she said, "but it would be the nice thing to do, Jack."

"If I don't have to do it," I said, "I don't want to do it."

His mom asked him to think about it overnight. "It's not just that he's a new kid, Mom," I answered. "He's deformed." "That's a terrible thing to say, Jack." "He is, Mom."

✦ 我不想做

Jack 的第一反应诚实得令人心疼——他知道 August 是谁,他不想做这件事。不是因为恶意,而是因为恐惧。

2
我记得那个画面
I Remember Seeing · "He wasn't wearing a mask"
回忆童年

Jack's first memory of August was when he was five or six, sitting on a bench outside Carvel on Amesfort Avenue. He turned to suck the ice cream from the bottom of his cone — and saw August sitting right next to him.

"I know it wasn't cool, but I kind of went 'Uhh!' when I saw him because I honestly got scared. I thought he was wearing a zombie mask or something."

His babysitter Veronica grabbed him and Jamie and rushed away. "Veronica, what was wrong with that kid?" I whispered. "Hush, boy!" she said, her voice angry. Jamie asked: "Is it Halloween?"

Veronica scolded them: "It was bad how we did that. Just getting up like that, like we'd just seen the devil. … It was very bad, us leaving like that. The momma knew what was going on."

"But we didn't mean it," I answered. "Jack, sometimes you don't have to mean to hurt someone to hurt someone. You understand?"

✦ 不需要故意伤害

Veronica 的话是 Jack 最早的道德课。不经意的反应有时比恶意的伤害更锋利——因为它让被伤害的人知道,自己的样子就是一种冒犯。

3
还有谁接到了电话
Who Else Did · "A kid like August doesn't stand a chance"
转变同理心

Jack asked who else Mr. Tushman called. Julian and Charlotte. "Julian!" I said. "Ugh. Why Julian? Julian's the biggest phony there is." "Well," said Mom, "at least Julian agreed to help this kid out. Got to give him credit for that." Jack didn't say anything because she was right.

Jack tried to explain what August looked like. He pointed to his cheeks: "His eyes are like down here. And he has no ears. And his mouth is like …"

Little brother Jamie walked in. "Oh, that kid?" said Jamie, his eyes opening wide. "He gave me a nightmare!! Remember, Mommy? That nightmare about the zombies from last year?"

Mom was horrified that Jamie had run away screaming from August. "Of course you could help it! I mean, honestly, he's just a little boy—just like you! Can you imagine how he felt to see you running away from him, Jamie, screaming?"

"It's just he's so ugly, Mommy," said Jamie.

Jack suddenly changed his mind. "The thing is, there are always going to be kids like Julian who are jerks. But if a little kid like Jamie, who's usually a nice enough kid, can be that mean, then a kid like August doesn't stand a chance in middle school."

✦ 他没有机会

Jack 改变主意的理由不是内疚,而是一种清醒的认知:如果连一个善良的小孩子都会这样对待 August,那他在中学里需要有人站在他身边。
