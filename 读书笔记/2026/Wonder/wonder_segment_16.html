1
我家不富裕
My Parents Are Not Rich · "The hobo who took the sled"
阶级羞辱

"My parents are not rich. I say this because people sometimes think that everyone who goes to private school is rich, but that isn't true with us."

Jack's dad was a teacher, mom a social worker. They lived on the top floor of a five-story walk-up on the "other" side of Broadway. "That's 'code' for the section of North River Heights where people don't want to park their cars."

At recess, Jack was hanging out with Julian, Henry, and Miles. Julian complained about going to Paris for Christmas. Miles bragged about an eight-hundred-dollar sled. "We should all go sledding and have a race down Skeleton Hill," I said. "That hill is so lame," answered Julian.

Then Miles said he'd left his "crappy old sled" by the rock at the bottom of the hill, and someone took it. "Maybe a hobo wanted to go sledding!" laughed Julian. "Hey, Jack, where are you going?" I had started to walk away. "I've got to get a book out of my locker," I lied. I just wanted to get away from them fast. I didn't want anyone to know that I was the 'hobo' who had taken the sled.

✦ 流浪汉的雪橇

Jack 视若珍宝的"闪电"雪橇,在 Miles 眼里不过是扔掉也不要的垃圾。阶级差异在这一刻如此锋利——Julian 的笑话刺中的不只是 Jack 的自尊,还有他对这段友情的焦虑。

2
我不是最优秀的学生
I'm Not the Greatest · "Bleeding Scream — it was August!"
顿悟内疚

Jack hated school—especially science. With August ex-friend, he couldn't copy notes anymore. While Ms. Rubin droned on about the science-fair project, his mind wandered. "I was thinking, We just finished the freakin' Egypt project, now we have to start a whole new thing?"

Then he thought of those pictures of melting ghost faces, mouths open wide, screaming. "And then all of a sudden this picture flew into my head, this memory, and I knew what Summer had meant by 'bleeding scream.' It's so weird how it all just came to me in this flash."

"Someone in homeroom had dressed up in a Bleeding Scream costume on Halloween. I remember seeing him a few desks away from me. And then I remember not seeing him again. Oh man. It was August!"

He'd been talking to Julian about August. "Oh man. Now I understood! I was so mean. I don't even know why. I'm not even sure what I said, but it was bad. I just was going along. I was stupid. I am stupid."

"Oh God. He was supposed to come as Boba Fett! I would never have said that stuff in front of Boba Fett. But that was him, that Bleeding Scream sitting at the desk looking over at us. The long white mask with the fake squirting blood. The mouth open wide. Like the ghoul was crying. That was him."

"I felt like I was going to puke."

✦ 那是他

顿悟的那一刻,Jack 才真正意识到 August 亲耳听到了那些话。Bleeding Scream 面具哭嚎的嘴巴,和 August 听到朋友背叛时的表情重叠在一起。

3
我一个字都没听进去
I Didn't Hear a Word · The punch
愤怒正义

After the realization, Jack couldn't hear a word Ms. Rubin said. Then she assigned partners: "Reid and Tristan, Maya and Max, Charlotte and Ximena, August and Jack."

August was already out the door. Julian sauntered over: "Looks like you and your best bud are partners. You must be so bummed you got stuck with him. You should tell Ms. Rubin you want to switch partners."

Before Jack could stop him, Julian raised his hand. "Ms. Rubin? Could we switch partners if we wanted to? Me and Jack had this science-fair project idea we wanted to work on together."

Jack cut in: "No, it's okay, Ms. Rubin. Bye!"

Julian ran after him in the hallway. "Why'd you do that? We could have been partners. You don't have to be friends with that freak if you don't want to be, you know. …" And that's when I punched him. Right in the mouth.

✦ 一拳

Jack 说不出为什么。但沉默的愤怒在那一刻找到了出口。这不是一时冲动——这是对整个秋天所有矛盾的爆发:对 August 的内疚、对 Julian 的厌恶、对自己的失望。

4
有些事就是说不清
Some Things You Just Can't Explain · Suspension
沉默保护

"Some things you just can't explain. You don't even try. You don't know where to start. All your sentences would jumble up like a giant knot if you opened your mouth."

In Mr. Tushman's office, Jack sat across the desk staring at a pumpkin picture on the wall. "Kids get expelled for this kind of thing, Jack! You have to explain yourself."

Jack's mom came from work. "Honestly, he's never hit anyone before. He's just not like that."

Mr. Tushman pushed: "I just want to know why." "It'll just make everything worse."

"If I told him that Julian had called August a freak, then he'd go talk to Julian about it, then Julian would tell him how I had badmouthed August, too, and everybody would find out about it."

Jack started to cry. Mr. Tushman gave him a two-day suspension over winter break. "Everything is going to be okay. I know you're a nice kid, Jack. And I know that sometimes even nice kids do dumb things, right?"

✦ 解释了反而更糟

Jack 不是不想说——他是没法说。因为他一旦开口,就会暴露自己的背叛。他不是在保护 Julian,他是在保护那个在万圣节说过可怕话的自己。
