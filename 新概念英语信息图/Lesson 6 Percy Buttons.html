02原文故事

I have just moved to a house in BridgeStreet. Yesterday a beggar knocked at my door.

He asked me for a meal and a glass of beer. In return for this, the beggarstood on his head and sang songs.

I gave him a meal. He ate the food and drank the beer.

Then he put a piece of cheese in his pocket and went away.

Later a neighbour told me about him. Every body knows him. His name is Percy Buttons.

He calls at every house in the street once a month and always asks for a meal and a glass of beer.

我刚刚搬进了大桥街的一所房子。昨天一个乞丐来敲我的门

问我要一顿饭和一杯啤酒。作为回报，那乞丐头顶地倒立起来，嘴里还唱着歌。

我给了他一顿饭。他把食物吃完，又喝了酒。

然后把一块乳酪装进衣袋里走了。

后来，一位邻居告诉了我他的情况。大家都认识他，他叫珀西.巴顿斯。

他每月对这条街上的每户人家光顾一次，总是请求给他一顿饭和一杯啤酒。
