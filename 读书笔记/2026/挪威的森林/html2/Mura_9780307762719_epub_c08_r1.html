1
割伤的手
The Cut Hand · Broken Glass, Towels Soaked in Red
事件 日常

HALFWAY THROUGH THAT WEEK I MANAGED TO CUT MY PALM OPEN on a piece of broken glass. I hadn't noticed that one of the glass partitions in a record shelf was cracked. I could hardly believe how much blood gushed out of me, turning the floor at my feet bright red. The store manager found a bunch of towels and tied them tightly over the wound. Then he made a telephone call to locate an all-night emergency room.

那周的中旬，我的手掌被一块碎玻璃划伤了。我没注意到唱片架的玻璃隔板有一块裂了。涌出的血量让我难以置信，脚下的地板被染成了鲜红色。唱片店经理找来一堆毛巾，紧紧地包扎了伤口，然后打电话找了一家夜间急诊医院。

He was a pretty useless guy most of the time, but this he did with great dispatch.

他大部分时间是个挺没用的人，但这次动作非常麻利。

Back at the record shop, the manager told me to go home: he would put me down as having worked. I took a bus to the dorm and went straight to Nagasawa's room. With my nerves on edge over the cut, I wanted to talk to somebody, and I felt I hadn't seen Nagasawa for a long time.

回到唱片店，经理让我回家休息，说照常算我上班。我坐公交车回了宿舍，直接去了永泽的房间。割伤让我神经紧张，想找人说说话，而且我已经很久没见到永泽了。

2
永泽通过考试
Nagasawa Passes · Foreign Ministry Exam, Spanish
对话 人物

"Oh, yeah, they posted the exam results the other day. I passed!"

"对了，前几天出成绩了。我通过了！"

"The Foreign Ministry exam?"

"外务省的考试？"

"Congratulations!" I said, and gave him my left hand to shake.

"恭喜！"我说，伸出左手和他握手。

"I'll give you this refrigerator when I get out of here," said Nagasawa. "You'd like to have it, wouldn't you? It's great for beer."

"我离开的时候把冰箱给你，"永泽说。"你想要吧？放啤酒最合适了。"

"The more languages you know the better. And I've got a knack for them. I taught myself French and it's practically perfect. Languages are like games. You learn the rules for one, and they all work the same way. Like women."

"语言越多人会越好。而且我有这方面的天赋。我自学了法语，已经近乎完美了。语言就像游戏。学会了一门的规则，其他都差不多。就像女人一样。"

"Anyhow, let's go out to eat sometime soon."

"总之，咱们找个时间出去吃顿饭。"

✦ 永泽的哲学

永泽考取了外务省，即将步入精英之路。他对世界有一套冷酷而系统的认知——语言如游戏、女人如语言。他的逻辑无懈可击，却令人不寒而栗。

3
高级餐厅的晚餐
Dinner at the French Restaurant · Azabu, Midnight Blue
场景 社交

NAGASAWA PICKED A FANCY French restaurant in a quiet backstreet of Azabu.

永泽选了一家位于麻布僻静小巷的高级法式餐厅。

Hatsumi arrived fifteen minutes later. She was carefully made up and wore gold earrings, a beautiful deep blue dress, and tasteful red pumps. When I complimented her on the color of the dress, she told me it was called midnight blue.

初美迟到了十五分钟。她精心化了妆，戴着金耳环，穿着美丽的深蓝色连衣裙和高雅的红色高跟鞋。我赞美她裙子的颜色时，她说这叫午夜蓝。

"What an elegant restaurant!" said Hatsumi.

"好雅致的餐厅！"初美说。

Nagasawa ordered a second bottle of wine when we had finished the first, and for himself he ordered a double scotch.

第一瓶喝完后，永泽又点了第二瓶葡萄酒，给自己加了一杯双份苏格兰威士忌。

Hatsumi then began talking about a girl she wanted to fix me up with. This was a perpetual topic for the two of us.

然后初美开始说起她想介绍给我的一个女生。这是我们之间永恒的话题。

✦ 三个人的晚餐

永泽、初美和"我"——这让人联想到木月、直子和"我"的三人组合。但永泽不是木月，初美不是直子。危险的气氛正在酝酿。

4
争吵
The Argument · Arrogance, Systems, Hurt
冲突 哲学

Nagasawa could get nasty when he was drunk, but tonight his nastiness was aimed at Hatsumi, not at me.

永泽喝醉时会变得很讨厌，但今晚他的恶意指向了初美，而不是我。

"What I can't stand is that arrogance of yours," said Hatsumi in a soft voice. "Whether you sleep with other women or not is beside the point. I've never really gotten angry at you for fooling around, have I?"

"我受不了的是你的傲慢，"初美轻声说。"你跟不跟别的女人上床是另一回事。我从来也没有真的为你拈花惹草生气过，对吧？"

"I get hurt," said Hatsumi. "Why am I not enough for you?"

"我很受伤，"初美说。"为什么我对你来说不够？"

Nagasawa kept silent for a moment and swirled the whiskey in his glass. "It's not that you're not enough for me. That's another phase, another question. It's just a hunger I have inside me. If I've hurt you, I'm sorry. But it's not a question of whether or not you're enough for me. I can only live with that hunger. That's the kind of man I am."

永泽沉默了一会儿，摇晃着杯中的威士忌。"不是你对我不够。那是另一个阶段、另一个问题。只是我内心有某种饥饿。如果我伤害了你，对不起。但这不是你是否足够的问题。我只能和那个饥饿共存。我就是这样的人。"

At last Hatsumi picked up her silverware and started eating her fish. "At least you shouldn't drag Toru into your 'games.'"

初美终于拿起餐具，开始吃鱼。"至少你不应该把渡边拖进你的'游戏'。"

✦ 不可弥合的裂痕

永泽与初美的根本分歧：永泽将一切纳入自己的"体系"——包括爱情和背叛。初美想要的只是平凡的幸福。这是全书最令人心碎的餐桌。

5
初美的台球
Pool with Hatsumi · Four-Ball, Grandfather's Legacy
台球 独处

"I want Toru to see me home."

"我要渡边送我回家。"

We found a pool hall nearby and went in. It was a small place at the far end of an alley. The two of us—Hatsumi in her chic dress and I in my blue blazer and regimental tie—clashed with the scruffy pool hall, but this didn't seem to concern Hatsumi at all as she chose and chalked her cue.

我们在附近找到一家台球厅走了进去。那是小巷尽头的一家小店。我们两个人——初美穿着时髦的连衣裙，我穿着蓝色运动外套和条纹领带——与这个邋遢的台球厅格格不入，但初美毫不在意地选了一根球杆、涂上滑石粉。

We played two rounds of four-ball. Hatsumi was as good as she had claimed to be, while my own game was hampered by the thick bandage I still wore on my cut hand. She crushed me.

我们打了两局四球。初美和她自己说的一样厉害，而我因为手上缠着厚厚的绷带，影响了发挥。她把我打得落花流水。

"My grandfather—my father's father—was an old playboy. He had a table in his house. I used to shoot pool with my brother just for fun, and when I got a little bigger my grandfather taught me the right moves."

"我爷爷——就是我父亲的父亲——是个老花花公子。他家里有台球桌。我小时候跟哥哥打着玩，长大一点后爷爷教了我正规打法。"

Watching her in action—her carefully set hair swept back out of her eyes, golden earrings sparkling, pumps set firmly on the floor, lovely, slender fingers pressing the felt as she took her shot—I felt as if her area of the scruffy pool parlor had been transformed into part of some elegant social event.

看着她打球的样子——精心梳理的头发掠开眼前，金耳环闪闪发光，高跟鞋稳稳地踩在地板上，纤细可爱的手指按在台面毛毡上瞄准——我觉得她所在的那片邋遢台球厅仿佛变成了某个优雅社交场合的一部分。

6
初美的公寓
Hatsumi's Apartment · Bandage, Beer, The Phone Call
独处 对话

Hatsumi's apartment was a fifteen-minute walk from Shibuya toward Ebisu.

初美的公寓距离涩谷往惠比寿方向步行十五分钟。

Setting a first-aid box on the table, she undid my bandage, checked to see that the wound was still sealed, put a little disinfectant on the area, and tied a new bandage over the cut. She did all this like an expert.

她把急救箱放在桌上，解开我的绷带，检查伤口是否闭合，涂了一点消毒药水，然后重新包扎好。她做这一切像专业人士一样娴熟。

"I used to do volunteer work at a hospital. Kind of like playing nurse. That's how I learned."

"我以前在医院做义工。有点像扮护士。就是这么学会的。"

She showed me pictures of the freshman girls in her club. She was right: several of them were cute.

她给我看了社团里大一女生的照片。她说得没错，有好几个确实可爱。

As I was standing in the entryway putting on my shoes, the telephone rang. Hatsumi looked at me, looked at the phone, and looked at me again. "Good night," I said, stepping outside. As I shut the door, I caught a glimpse of Hatsumi picking up the receiver. It was the last time I ever saw her.

我站在玄关穿鞋时，电话响了。初美看看我，看看电话，又看看我。"晚安，"我说着退出门外。关上门的一刹那，我看见初美拿起听筒。那是我最后一次见到她。

✦ 最后的告别

这是初美在全书中的最后一次出场。多年后"我"得知：永泽去德国两年后她结了婚，又过了两年——她用剃刀割断了手腕。

7
关于爱情的建议
Advice on Love · An Elder Sister, Happiness
对话 哲思

"Well, if I were you, I'd leave him. I'd find someone with a more normal way of looking at things and live happily ever after."

"如果我是你，我会离开他。找一个看事情更正常的人，从此幸福地生活下去。"

"There's no way in hell you can be happy with that guy. The way he lives, it never crosses his mind to try to make himself happy or to make others happy. Staying with him can only wreck your nervous system."

"你跟着那个家伙绝对不可能幸福。他的生活方式里，从来就没有让自己幸福或让别人幸福的念头。跟他在一起只会毁掉你的神经。"

"You know, when we were shooting pool before, something popped into my mind. I was an only child, but the whole time I was growing up I never once felt deprived or wished I had brothers or sisters. I was satisfied being alone. But all of a sudden, shooting pool with you, I had this feeling like I wished I had had an elder sister like you."

"你知道吗，刚才打台球时，我突然冒出一个念头。我是独生子，从小到大从来没有觉得缺少什么，也没想过要兄弟姐妹。我很满足于一个人。但和你打台球时，我突然觉得——要是有一个像你这样的姐姐就好了。"

"All I want for you," I said, blushing, "is for you to be happy."

"我只希望你幸福，"我红着脸说。

"But anyway, Toru, I'm not the smartest girl in the world. If anything, I'm sort of on the stupid side, and old-fashioned. I couldn't care less about 'systems' and 'responsibility.' All I want is to get married and have a man I love hold me in his arms every night and make kids. That's plenty for me. It's all I want out of life."

"不过呢，渡边，我不是世界上最有头脑的女孩子。倒不如说我有点笨，比较传统。我对什么'体系'啊'责任'啊一点都不在乎。我只想结婚，被心爱的男人每晚抱在怀里，生孩子。对我来说这就够了。这就是我对人生的全部要求。"

✦ 姐姐

"我"第一次对初美说了真心话：你像姐姐。初美笑着回答——这是"过去一年里听到的最好听的话"。这个简单的愿望让她如此动人。

8
木月最后的台球
Kizuki's Last Game · Cushion Shot, Pepsi Machine
回忆 死亡

I still remember the last shot Kizuki took that day—the day he died. It was a difficult cushion shot that I never expected him to make. Luck seemed to be with him, though: the shot was absolutely perfect, and the white and red balls hardly made a sound as they brushed each other on the green felt for the last point of the game. It was such a beautiful shot, I can still bring back a vivid image of it to this day.

我还记得木月那天最后一杆——他死的那天。那是一杆很难的库边球，我从没想过他能打进去。但运气似乎站在他那边：那一杆完美极了，白球和红球在绿色台面上轻轻相擦，拿下最后一分，几乎没有发出声响。那么漂亮的一杆，至今我仍能清晰地回忆起那个画面。

The night I played pool with Hatsumi, though, the thought of Kizuki never crossed my mind until the first game ended, and this came as a major shock to me.

然而，和初美打台球的那个晚上，直到第一局结束我才想起木月——这让我大为震惊。

I felt guilty that I hadn't thought of Kizuki right away, as if I had somehow abandoned him.

我为自己没有立刻想起木月而感到内疚，好像我在某种程度上抛弃了他。

I'm going to turn twenty soon. Part of what Kizuki and I shared when we were sixteen and seventeen has already vanished, and no amount of crying is going to bring that back.

我马上就要二十岁了。木月和我十六七岁时共同拥有的东西，有一部分已经消失了，无论怎么哭都回不来了。

✦ 遗忘的愧疚

和初美打台球时，"我"几乎忘了木月——直到看见自动售货机才想起曾经赌饮料的时光。两年半了，遗忘本身就是一种背叛。

9
给直子的信
Letter to Naoko · Kind of Blue, Rainy Sunday
书信 内心

I SAT AT MY DESK to write my Sunday morning letter to Naoko, drinking coffee from a big cup and listening to old Miles Davis records. A fine rain was falling outside, while my room had the chill of an aquarium.

我坐在桌前写星期天早上给直子的信，一边喝着大杯咖啡，一边听着迈尔斯·戴维斯的老唱片。窗外下着细雨，房间里有着水族馆般的凉意。

I began by telling Naoko how I had given my right hand a nasty cut while working in the record store, then went on to say that Nagasawa, Hatsumi, and I had had a sort of celebration the night before for Nagasawa's having passed his Foreign Ministry exam. I described the restaurant and the food. The meal was a good one, I said, but the atmosphere changed to something uncomfortable partway through.

我开头告诉直子我在唱片店工作的时候右手受了伤，又说起永泽、初美和我前一天晚上为永泽通过外务省考试搞了个小型庆祝。我描述了餐厅和食物。我说菜很好吃，但气氛中途变得不舒服了。

I wondered if I should write about Kizuki in connection with having shot pool with Hatsumi and decided to go ahead. It was something I ought to write about, I felt.

我犹豫要不要写关于和初美打台球时想起木月的事，最后决定写出来。我觉得这是应该写的事。

"Two and a half years have gone by since it happened, and Kizuki is still seventeen years old. Not that this means my memory of him has faded. The things that his death gave rise to are still there, bright and clear, inside me, some of them even clearer than when they were new."

"事情过去两年半了，木月仍然十七岁。但这不意味着我对他的记忆褪色了。他的死所引发的东西，仍然清晰明亮地留在我心中，有些甚至比当初还要清晰。"

"I think of you now more than ever. It's raining today. Rainy Sundays make it hard for me. When it rains, I can't do laundry, which means I can't do ironing. I can't go walking, and I can't lie down on the roof. About all I can do is put the record player on auto repeat and listen to Kind of Blue over and over while I watch the rain falling in the quadrangle."

"我现在比任何时候都想你。今天下雨。下雨的星期天让我难过。下雨就不能洗衣服，也就不能熨衣服。不能散步，也不能躺在屋顶。我唯一能做的就是让唱机自动反复播放，一边听着《Kind of Blue》一遍又一遍，一边看着雨水落在院子里。"

"As I wrote to you earlier, I don't wind my spring on Sundays. That's why this letter is so damn long. I'm stopping now. I'm going to the dining hall for lunch. Good-bye."

"就像我之前写给你的，星期天我不上发条。所以这封信才这么长。不写了。我去食堂吃午饭。再见。"

✦ 星期天的信

第八章以给直子的信结束。"我"在信中说：木月永远十七岁，而"我"即将二十岁。有些东西消失了，但直子——她是世界上唯一能理解这一切的人。
