Wonder段十九 · August · 助听器与告别

R. J. Palacio
August 回到主视角。科学展上所有家长的目光像指南针指向他的脸。战争在二月达到顶峰——Julian 的纸条和 Beulah 的回击。August 终于戴上了助听器,世界变得"明亮"了。但 Via 隐藏的秘密在晚餐时爆发——她不想让 August 出现在她的新学校。争吵过后,老狗 Daisy 病危。那个深夜,August 躺在妈妈身边问:天堂里还需要脸吗?
1
北极
North Pole · "All those eyes are compasses, and I'm the North Pole."
目光比喻

The spud lamp won an A at the science fair — Jack's first A all year. All the projects were set up in the gym, same as the Egyptian Museum, but this time parents wandered from table to table. "Here's the math: sixty kids in the grade equals sixty sets of parents — and that doesn't even include grandparents. So that's a minimum of one hundred and twenty pairs of eyes that find their way over to me. … All those eyes are compasses, and I'm like the North Pole to them."

August still hated school events with parents. The Thanksgiving Sharing Festival was the worst. The winter concert was awful — singing in the chorus, on display. "It's like when you go outside and it's drizzling a little. You don't put on boots for a drizzle. But when it's a huge gym full of parents, the drizzle becomes this total hurricane. Everyone's eyes hit you like a wall of water."

Mom and Dad hung around his table with Jack's parents and Summer's mom. Julian's parents clustered with Henry's and Miles's parents. "It's kind of funny how parents actually end up forming the same little groups their kids form." Mom thought that was a funny observation. "I guess it's true that like seeks like."

✦ 指南针与北极

August 用一个完美的类比解释了"被注视"的体验——他不是在抱怨,只是在陈述物理定律。而"同类相吸"不仅适用于孩子,也适用于家长:大人的小团体不过是孩子世界的镜像。

2
Auggie Doll
The Auggie Doll · "You are so funny, August!"
战争转折

The "war" peaked in February. Julian left notes in their lockers: "You stink, big cheese!" "Nobody likes you anymore!" August got "Freak!" and "Get out of our school, orc!" Summer wanted to report it, but they thought that was snitching. Instead they wrote back as a made-up person named Beulah: "You're so pretty, Julian! I love you. Will you marry me?" and "Love your hair! XOX Beulah" — Beulah had gross habits and a crush on Julian, who looked like he belonged in a KidzBop commercial.

Julian and his goons played tricks on Jack — stole his gym shorts, Monkey-in-the-Middled his worksheet. Jack never let them see he was upset. But by March the girls were sick of the war, and even Amos started to turn. When Julian spread a rumor that Jack hired a "hit man," people laughed at him. By the end of March, only Miles and Henry were left.

"People even joke around with me now sometimes." August saw Maya writing on Uglydoll stationery and said: "Did you know the guy who created the Uglydolls based them on me?" Maya believed him for a second, then laughed harder than he'd ever seen. The next day she left an Uglydoll key chain on his chair: "For the nicest Auggie Doll in the world! XO Maya." Six months ago that never would have happened.

✦ Beulah 的情书

面对恶意,August 和 Jack 选择了幽默——他们创造了一个恶心又浪漫的虚构人物 Beulah 来讽刺 Julian。而 Auggie Doll 这个瞬间是全书的转折点:当一个孩子不再害怕他的外表,幽默感就成为了超级力量。

3
Lobot
Lobot · "I am not wearing that thing."
身体星际大战

Doctors had always said August would need hearing aids someday. The ocean sound in his head had been getting louder, drowning out voices like he was underwater. He'd hoped to make it through fifth grade without them — but the October audiology test flunked him.

Of all his features, August hated his ears the most. "They're like tiny closed fists on the sides of my face. Too low on my head. Like squashed pieces of pizza dough sticking out of the top of my neck." The ear doctor showed him the device: heavy-duty headband with earbuds. "I'll look like Lobot!" August groaned. The doctor — a Star Wars fan — smiled: "Lobot is cool. It's not like we're saying you're going to look like Jar Jar."

He looked in the mirror. His eyes teared up. "Do I really have to wear this, Mom? I hate them. They don't make any difference!" The doctor hadn't turned them on yet. "Wait until you hear the difference: you'll want to wear them." "No I won't!" And then he turned them on.

✦ 最后的尊严

August 已经接受了自己的脸,但耳朵是他最后的防线——它们是"被捏扁的披萨面团",而助听器像天线一样戳在两侧。这是全书最令人心痛的一幕之一,不是因为外貌描写,而是因为一个十岁孩子说"I hate them"时的绝望。

4
听见光明
Hearing Brightly · "It sounds … lighter."
奇迹接纳

"How can I describe what I heard when the doctor turned on my hearing aids? The ocean just wasn't living inside my head anymore. It was gone. I could hear sounds like shiny lights in my brain." He thought of a room where one lightbulb doesn't work — you don't realize how dark it is until someone changes it. "I don't know if there's a word that means the same as 'bright' in terms of hearing, but I wish I knew one, because my ears were hearing brightly now."

"I don't hear that noise anymore. It's so quiet in my ears." The white noise was gone. "It sounds … lighter." The doctor adjusted the volume and showed him the controls. His hair covered most of the headband. Just the tubing peeked out.

The first day at school, no one made a big deal. Summer was happy for him. Jack said he looked like an FBI agent. Mr. Browne asked politely if he needed things repeated. "Now that I look back, I don't know why I was so stressed about it all this time. Funny how sometimes you worry a lot about something and it turns out to be nothing."

✦ 声音的亮度

"Lighter" 和 "bright"——August 用视觉词汇描述听觉,因为这是第一次他能用比喻来理解一个没有"白色的噪音"的世界。那个一直存在于他脑海里的海洋消失了。最动人的是最后那句:我们担忧的事情,大多数都不会发生。

5
Via 的秘密
Via's Secret · "But what is with you lately?"
爆发秘密

Spring break ended. Mom found out Via hadn't told her about the school play. She was mad — really mad. August heard them yelling from Via's room. His bionic Lobot ears picked up: "But what is with you lately, Via? You're moody and taciturn and secretive!" Via screamed back: "What is so wrong with my not telling you about a stupid play? I don't even have a speaking part in it!"

Mom mentioned Justin. Via snapped: "No! Actually, I don't want you to come!" Then quieter, more cutting: "You've been really good about leaving me alone my whole life, so why you choose high school to suddenly be interested I have no idea." Then silence — even his bionic Lobot ears couldn't pick up a signal.

✦ 被忽视的长女

Via 那句"你一直都很擅长不管我"说出了全书最隐晦的真相——在一个需要特殊关注的家庭里,正常的孩子往往被自动忽略了。这不是恶意的忽略,但仍然造成了伤害。

6
我的洞穴
My Cave · "I know what's going on!"
崩溃真相

Dinner was quiet. Daisy was sick — appointment at the vet in the morning. August asked if they were going to see Justin's play. Mom and Via danced around it. "So I'm not invited?" Via accused him of getting bored. Mom said Dad would go, she'd stay home. "What? You're going to punish me for being honest by not going?"

August exploded. "You think I don't know what's going on? You just don't want your brand-new fancy high school friends to know your brother's a freak!" Mom grabbed his arm. He pulled away. "Stop treating me like a baby! I'm not retarded! I know what's going on!"

He ran to his room, slammed the door hard enough to crumble bits of wall. He piled his stuffed animals and pillows over his face — a little cave. He waited for Mom to come. She didn't. Ten minutes. Half an hour. Finally the door opened. It was Via. "She didn't even bother coming over to my bed, and she didn't come in softly like I thought she would. She came in quickly."

✦ 枕头的洞穴

这是 August 第一次真正对 Via 发火——不是因为他受伤了,而是因为他太聪明了,聪明到无法被谎言安抚。"I'm not retarded"是一个十岁孩子对这个世界最绝望的声明。而 Mom 半小时没有进来——这是全书最意味深长的沉默。也许她终于决定让 Via 来处理一次。

7
再见
Goodbye · "Not everything in the world is about you, Auggie!"
告别Daisy

"I'm not apologizing!" "This isn't about you! Not everything in the world is about you, Auggie! Now hurry up. Daisy's sick. Mom's taking her to the emergency vet. Come say goodbye."

Daisy lay sideways on the kitchen floor, legs straight out, panting heavily. Mom was kneeling beside her, stroking her head. "I think it might be time we say goodbye to Daisy, Auggie. I'm sorry." "No!" The taxi arrived. Mom picked up Daisy like a huge droopy baby. August stood in front of the door. "Please, no, Mommy?"

Via moved him aside. "Say goodbye to Daisy, kids," Mom said, tears streaming. Via kissed Daisy's nose: "I love you. I love you so much." August whispered into her ear: "Bye, little girlie … I love you. …"

Mom carried Daisy down the stoop. Just before closing the taxi door, she looked up and gave a little wave. "I don't think I've ever seen her look sadder." "I love you, Mommy! I'm sorry, Mommy!" Mom blew a kiss. The car left. Via closed the door, looked at him a second, then hugged him very, very tight while they both cried a million tears.

✦ 眼泪的和解

"Not everything in the world is about you"——这句话在 Daisy 病危的时刻变得无比正确又无比残酷。所有的争吵在生死面前化为乌有。August 和 Via 在门廊后紧紧拥抱——他们需要的不是道歉,只是一个共同哭的理由。

8
Daisy 的玩具
Daisy's Toys · "Daddy was right. We shouldn't have let her get this bad."
哀悼家庭

Justin came over half an hour later. He hugged August: "Sorry, Auggie." Via and August had gathered all of Daisy's toys into a little pile on the coffee table. They just stared at it. For the first time, Via told August the truth: Daisy had been sick for months — throwing up, vet visits — Mom and Dad hadn't wanted to worry them. Daisy bit Mom when she tried to touch her stomach.

"I'm sorry, Auggie," she said softly. "I'm really sorry about everything, okay? You forgive me? You know how much I love you, right?" August nodded. Somehow that fight didn't matter much now.

Mom and Dad came home two hours later. The second they opened the door and Daisy wasn't with them, everyone knew. Dad told them: the vet found a huge mass in her stomach. They didn't want her to suffer. Dad held her in his arms with her legs straight up in the air, and they kissed her goodbye while the vet put a needle into her leg. After about a minute, she died in Daddy's arms. "So peaceful. She wasn't in any pain at all. Like she was just going to sleep." A couple of times while he talked, Dad's voice got trembly. He cleared his throat.

Later, August saw Dad sitting on the edge of the bed, taking off his socks. At first he thought Dad was laughing — shoulders shaking — then he put his palms on his eyes and August realized he was crying. "It was the quietest crying I've ever heard. Like a whisper." He thought maybe Dad was whisper-crying because he didn't want anyone to hear. So he left. He went to Via's room and saw Mom lying next to Via, whispering to her while she cried. So he went to bed alone, put on his pajamas without being told, took off his hearing aids, and "imagined Daisy snuggling with me, her big wet tongue licking my face all over like it was her favorite face in the world."

✦ 爸爸的哭泣

全书最安静的悲伤时刻——August 第一次看到爸爸哭。Nate Pullman 一直以幽默和温暖支撑家庭,但失去了老狗之后,他坐在床边无声地啜泣,肩膀颤抖,用手掌捂住眼睛。而 August 懂得离开,给爸爸留下尊严。

9
天堂
Heaven · "You don't need your eyes to love, right?"
信仰温柔

August woke up in the dark and crawled into Mom and Dad's bed. Mom scooted over. He apologized for what he'd said at dinner. "Is Via ashamed of me?" "No, honey, no. She's just adjusting to a new school." "I know." "I know you know." Then he asked: "Mommy, is Daisy with Grans now?" "I think so." "Are they in heaven?" "Yes."

Then the question that had been building his whole life: "Do people look the same when they get to heaven?" "I don't know. I don't think so." "Then how do people recognize each other?" Mom's answer came through the darkness: "You don't need your eyes to love, right? You just feel it inside you. That's how it is in heaven. It's just love, and no one forgets who they love."

She fell asleep. August lay awake, listening to Mom and Dad sleep, imagining Via down the hall. He wondered if Daisy was sleeping in heaven right then, if she was dreaming about him. "And I wondered how it would feel to be in heaven someday and not have my face matter anymore. Just like it never, ever mattered to Daisy."

✦ 不再重要的脸

这是全书的哲学核心——一个十岁孩子在深夜问出了关于来世最诚实的问题。Isabel 的回答朴素而完美:"爱不需要眼睛。"而 August 最后的想象——在 Daisy 眼中他的脸从来都不重要——是他第一次把"不介意外表"当成祝福而不是事实陈述。

10
替角
Understudy · "Why do they call it understudy?"
戏剧家人

Via brought home three tickets to the play. The fight was never mentioned again. Before she left for school, she hugged August and told him she loved him and was proud to be his sister. It was August's first time in Via's new high school. "The only really bad thing about my bionic Lobot hearing aids was the fact that I couldn't wear a baseball cap anymore." He kept his head down walking through the bright hallways.

They found seats in the fifth row. Mom forgot her glasses — as usual. August saw Justin's picture in the program. "How come there's no picture of Via?" "She's an understudy." August asked three times what "understudy" meant. Mom and Dad whispered about Miranda's father getting remarried. "Daddy, can you please not call me Auggie Doggie anymore?" Dad smiled and gave a thumbs-up.

The play started. Justin sat onstage tuning his fiddle, opening with the stage manager's monologue. "I knew right then and there that I was going to like the play. It wasn't like other school plays I've been to. This was grown-up seeming, and I felt smart sitting there watching it."

When Emily walked onstage, Mom squinted: "That's Miranda." August corrected her: "It's Via." Mom lurched forward. "Oh my God!" Dad was already smiling. "I know. Shhh!"

✦ Auggie Doggie

在走进灯火通明的剧院之前,August 悄悄对爸爸说:别再叫我 Auggie Doggie 了。他正在长大——不只是年龄,而是他决定不再接受那个可以被昵称保护的童年身份。而爸爸的微笑和拇指,是同意。

11
结局
The Ending · "I think there should be a rule that everyone in the world should get a standing ovation at least once in their lives."
感动重逢

The play was amazing. Via-as-Emily delivered her goodbye: "Good-by, Good-by world! Good-by, Grover's Corners … Mama and Papa. Good-by to clocks ticking and Mama's sunflowers. And food and coffee. And new-ironed dresses and hot baths … and sleeping and waking up. Oh, earth, you're too wonderful for anybody to realize you!" Via was actually crying — real tears rolling down her cheeks. "It was totally awesome."

When Via and Justin appeared for the final bow, the whole audience rose. "I think there should be a rule that everyone in the world should get a standing ovation at least once in their lives." Backstage was chaos — crowds, congratulations. Dad lifted Via off the floor. Mom was screaming. Justin got hugs. Mr. Davenport froze when he saw August, but Justin pulled him away. People kept bumping into August, giving him the one-two look. He got dizzy. The voices were so loud it hurt. He tried to turn down his Lobot ears but turned them louder by mistake. He couldn't see Mom or Dad or Via anywhere. "Via? Mommy!"

Suddenly someone picked him up from behind. "Look who's here!" She hugged him tight. He thought it was Via, but when he turned around: "Hey, Major Tom!" "Miranda!" He gave her the tightest hug he could give.

✦ Major Tom

这段话值得用最慢的速度读一遍——因为最后的拥抱者是 Miranda,不是 Via。她完全没有犹豫就抱起了 August,叫他 Major Tom(源自 Bowie 的歌,和 Via 之前的"Major Tom"章节呼应)。在所有人"不小心"避开他的时候,一个他不常见到的人第一个找到了他。
