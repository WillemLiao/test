Wonder段二一 · August · 自然之旅

R. J. Palacio
五年级的自然保护区的三天两夜之旅。这是 August 第一次离开家过夜——他偷偷塞进熊玩偶,在天亮前又把它留给了妈妈。没有 Julian 的营地简直完美:篝火、星空、湿袜子大战。但在露天电影之夜,一场去树林里小便的决定,把他们带到了七个陌生八年级学生的面前。
1
五年级自然之旅
The Fifth-Grade Nature Retreat · "I've never slept away from home before."
第一次离家

Every spring the fifth graders went to Broarwood Nature Reserve in Pennsylvania — three days, two nights, cabin bunk beds, campfires, s'mores, long walks through the woods. All the kids were excited. Except August. "It's not even that I'm not excited, because I kind of am — it's just I've never slept away from home before and I'm kind of nervous."

Most kids his age had sleepovers. Not him — not unless you counted hospital stays, but even then Mom or Dad stayed. When he was little, there were medical issues — trache tube, feeding tube. But later, he just never felt like sleeping anywhere else. The one time he tried — at Christopher's house at age eight — he panicked at midnight and Dad drove all the way back to Bridgeport at 1 a.m. They didn't get home until 3 a.m. "So my one and only sleepover, up until now, was pretty much of a disaster." On the other hand, he was really excited.

✦ 从未在外过夜

August 十岁了,从未在朋友家过夜——气管套管、喂食管、午夜恐慌……他的童年被医疗和恐惧分割成一个个不能离开父母的碎片。这次自然之旅不仅是一次旅行,更是他第一次作为一个普通孩子体验"离开"。

2
被记住的事
Known For · "I do know what I'm really known for."
身份星战

August asked Mom for a new duffel bag — no Star Wars logos. "As much as I love Star Wars, I don't want that to be what I'm known for." Everyone in middle school was known for something: Reid for marine life, Amos for baseball, Charlotte for being in a TV commercial. The two Maxes would never live down their Dungeons & Dragons obsession. August was trying to ease out of the Star Wars thing. "I'm not sure what I want to be known for, but it's not that." He paused: "That's not exactly true: I do know what I'm really known for. But there's nothing I can do about that. A Star Wars duffel bag I could do something about."

✦ 无法改变的事

最心酸的一句话出现在最平淡的章节里。August 可以对星战背包做点什么,但他知道自己真正"出名"的原因是他无法改变的。这不是自怜,这是十岁孩子对世界规则最清醒的认知。

3
打包
Packing · "I won't need Baboo, but if you miss me, you can cuddle with him yourself."
告别成长

Mom helped pack — plain blue rolling duffel, no logos. "What if I can't sleep at night?" "Take a book." "What if I have a nightmare?" "Your teachers will be there. And Jack. And your friends." August asked to bring Baboo — his childhood stuffed bear — hidden deep in the bag where no one could see. Mom noticed the Empire Strikes Back poster was gone. "I'm trying to, you know, change my image a bit." She smiled.

Bug spray. Sunscreen. Take hearing aids off before swimming. Rain poncho. "Suppose I hate it there?" "You're going to have a great time, Auggie." She wanted to lie down with him, but he said: "You don't need to put me to bed tonight, Mom. I'll read on my own till I get sleepy." She squeezed his hand and kissed it. He read The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe — "though the Witch knew the Deep Magic, there is a magic deeper still which she did not know."

✦ 不需要哄睡了

August 在自然之旅前夜悄悄完成了一个仪式:他告诉妈妈"你今晚不用哄我睡了"。这不是一次过夜旅行——这是一个孩子从童年踏入少年门槛的时刻。而 Baboo——藏在行李箱最深处——是他在门槛上最后一步的犹豫。

4
黎明
Daybreak · "Dear Mom, I won't need Baboo."
幽灵独立

August woke up before dawn. In the darkness, he saw Daisy sitting near his bed — or a shadow that looked just like her. "It didn't make me sad to see her at all: it just filled me up with nice feelings inside." She was gone after a second. He put on his hearing aids and the world woke up — garbage trucks, birds, Mom's alarm beeping. "Daisy's ghost made me feel super strong inside, knowing wherever I am, she'd be there with me."

He went to his desk and wrote a note. Then he opened his bag, fished out Baboo, laid him in bed, and taped the note to his chest: "Dear Mom, I won't need Baboo, but if you miss me, you can cuddle with him yourself. XO Auggie."

✦ 给妈妈的熊

把 Baboo 留给妈妈,而不是带走——这是 August 在这趟旅行开始之前就已经完成的最勇敢的事。Daisy 的幻影在黎明前出现,给了他力量,也给了他告别。那只熊从此躺在妈妈的床上,成了他们之间最私密的仪式。

5
第一天
Day One · "I loved hanging out by the campfire after dark."
自然自由

The bus ride flew by. August sat by the window, Jack next to him. Julian wasn't on the bus — he ditched because the trip was "dorky." "I got totally pumped because dealing with Julian for three days in a row — and two nights — was a major reason that I was nervous about this whole trip."

They arrived at noon. Cabin bunk beds — August won the top bunk at rock-paper-scissors. "Woo-hoo." A two-hour guided nature hike through real woods — giant trees, tangles of leaves, howls and chirps. His Lobot hearing aids made him hear better than anyone. It started raining; they had a wet-sock fight in the cabin. Then Ping-Pong, Pac-Man, and a real campfire cookout with the best hot dogs he'd ever tasted. "I loved the way bits of fire dust would float up and disappear into the night air. And how the fire lit up people's faces. … And how the woods were so dark that you couldn't see anything around you, and you'd look up and see a billion stars in the sky." He fell asleep before his head hit the pillow.

✦ 十亿颗星星

August 第一次体验到没有父母、没有医院、没有恐惧的快乐。这是全书最纯粹的段落之一——湿袜子大战、Pac-Man、篝火和棉花糖。他只是一个在森林里仰望星空的普通男孩。

6
露天电影之夜
The Fairgrounds · "I could understand why ancient people used to think the world was flat."
日落期待

Day two: horseback riding, rappelling trees, then a bus ride to the fairgrounds for outdoor movie night. August wrote a letter home he'd read aloud later. At the fairgrounds, the sunset was impossible — "It looked like someone had taken sidewalk chalk and smudged the colors across the sky with their fingers." In that huge open field, he understood why ancient people thought the world was flat and the sky a dome closing over it.

They laid out sleeping bags in front of the giant screen. Jack and August failed to win anything at the carnival stalls, but Amos won a yellow hippo for Ximena. "That was the big gossip: the jock and the brainiac." They played Pictionary as the sun went down. Then all the stadium lights went on at once — "like Close Encounters when the alien ship lands" — and everyone cheered.

✦ 粉笔涂抹的天空

August 至今为止最美的描写——用 sidewalk chalk 来形容日落,用 Close Encounters 来形容灯光亮起。他不仅是个经历过苦难的孩子,也是一个正在发现世界之美的人。

7
善待自然
Be Kind to Nature · "The hills are alive …"
音乐之声妈妈

The announcement welcomed three schools, then played the movie: The Sound of Music. August immediately started clapping — it was Via's all-time favorite. Kids from other schools booed and hissed. Someone threw a soda can at the screen. "I saw Mr. Tushman stand up and look in the direction of the can thrower, though I knew he couldn't see anything in the dark."

The movie started. Maria twirled on the mountaintop. August put on his yellow Montauk hoodie, adjusted his hearing aids, leaned against his backpack. "The hills are alive …"

✦ 音乐之声与汽水罐

一个美好的夜晚被远处的汽水罐打断了——就像这个故事的隐喻:即使在最平和的时候,敌意也会从黑暗中飞来。但 August 选择了关注 Maria 和山的旋律,而不是那个罐子。

8
森林在呼吸
The Woods Are Alive · "I've got to pee."
危险决定

Somewhere around "You are sixteen, going on seventeen," Jack nudged August. "Dude, I've got to pee." They hopscotched over kids and sleeping bags. Huge line for the toilets. "Forget this, I'll just find a tree." Jack headed past the orange cones toward the woods. August followed. They had no flashlights.

They passed Henry, Miles, and Amos coming back from the woods. "Be careful of the bears!" shouted Henry. The woods were loud — chirps, croaks, a wall of noise. Then "loud snaps not far from us, almost like cap gun pops, that definitely weren't insect noises." Far away, like another world: Raindrops on roses and whiskers on kittens. Jack did his business. August peed on the nearest tree. They smelled firecrackers. "Let's go."

✦ 橘色圆锥筒

工作人员明确警告过:不要越过橙色锥筒。Jack 和 August 越过了。这是全书最关键的一个"错误决定":因为一个男孩不想排长队上厕所,他们走进了那片不该进入的黑暗。六个中学生正在那里等着。

9
外星人
Alien · "Leave him alone, okay?"
暴力恐惧

They walked right into a group of six kids from another school — seventh graders, smoking, setting off firecrackers. A flashlight pointed at August's face. A girl started screaming. "No freakin' way, man! No freakin' way!" They half-laughed, half-covered their eyes. "It was only then that I realized that the flashlight was pointed right at my face, and what they were talking about — screaming about — was me."

Jack tried to pull August away. A kid named Eddie cut them off, shining the light in August's face. "What happened to your face?" "I didn't know we were watching Lord of the Rings tonight! Look, guys, it's Gollum!" Jack pushed the flashlight away. Eddie shoved him to the ground. August stepped in front of Jack: "We're a lot smaller than you guys …" Eddie: "Are you talking to me, Freddie Krueger? I don't think you want to mess with me, you ugly freak."

Then a new voice: "Leave them alone, dude." Amos, with Miles and Henry behind him.

✦ Gollum, Alien, Orc, Freddie Krueger

August 在一个晚上被叫了四个名字——没有一个是他自己的名字。Eddie 的每一句侮辱都在重复他整个童年听过的每一个绰号。但这次他有 Jack,有 Amos——以及黑暗里正在赶来的同盟。

10
黑暗中的声音
Voices in the Dark · "It was cool how you stood your ground, little dude."
盟友转折

Eddie grabbed August's hood and yanked him backward onto a rock — hard fall, bloody elbow. "Amos rammed into the Eddie guy like a monster truck and they both fell down." Someone pulled August up by his sleeve: "Run!" His sweatshirt ripped as two people pulled him in opposite directions. Henry had hold of his arm, dragging him through the pitch-black field. They ran until their stomachs hurt.

In the dark, they realized they'd lost Eddie's group. Amos's lip was bleeding. They were in the cornfields. Then Jack said: "That was really cool of you guys to come back for us. Really cool. Thanks." High-fives all around. Amos looked at August: "It was cool how you stood your ground, little dude." Miles and Henry high-fived him too. Then Jack noticed: "Your hearing aids are gone!" August touched his ears — the band was gone. That's why he felt underwater. He started crying — big crying, the waterworks — hiding his face in his arm. "The guys were really nice to me, though. They patted me on the back." Amos put both arms around him like his dad would have and let him cry.

✦ 玉米地里的拥抱

曾经是"Julian 的跟班"的 Amos 在此刻成了 August 的保护者。在黑暗的玉米地里,一个曾经孤立过他的男孩用父亲的方式抱住了他,让他哭。这比任何演讲都更能说明"选择善良"的力量——它甚至能改变已经站队的人。

11
皇帝卫队
The Emperor's Guard · "Ground Control to Major Tom."
保护卫队

They backtracked through the grass — too dark to find the hearing aids. No sign of the seventh graders. "Are we going to report them?" They looked at August. He shook his head. "Okay," said Amos, "but, little dude, don't walk around here alone again, okay? If you need to go somewhere, tell us and we'll go with you."

Back near the screen, August pulled his torn hoodie over his head, kept his face down. Without his hearing aids, he felt miles under the earth — like the song Miranda used to sing: "Ground Control to Major Tom, your circuit's dead, there's something wrong …" He noticed as they walked: "Amos had stayed right next to me. And Jack was close on the other side of me. And Miles was in front of us and Henry was in back of us. They were surrounding me as we walked through the crowds of kids. Like I had my own emperor's guard."

✦ 五人方阵

Amos 在前,Jack 在左,Henry 在后,Miles 在右——五个男孩排成一个方阵穿过人群,保护那个没有助听器、头罩撕裂的孩子。August 把它叫作"皇帝的卫队"。他确实不记得第七个单词是什么颜色的墨镜,但他记得这个队形。

12
睡眠
Sleep · "To everybody else it's just an ordinary night."
创伤与一只羊

Back at their spot in front of the screen, nobody had even noticed they were gone. "It's so weird how that can be, how you could have a night that's the worst in your life, but to everybody else it's just an ordinary night." On his calendar, this would be one of the most horrific days of his life — this and the day Daisy died. But for the rest of the world, this was just an ordinary day. Maybe someone won the lottery today.

Amos, Miles, and Henry went back to their group. But something had changed. August could see them whispering, looking over at him. News spreads fast. On the bus back to camp, even girls he didn't know asked if he was okay. Mr. Tushman found out — torn sweatshirt, bloody elbow. In the first-aid office, August told them he couldn't remember the faces. "Which wasn't true. It's their faces I kept seeing every time I closed my eyes to sleep. The look of total horror on the girl's face when she first saw me. The way the kid with the flashlight, Eddie, looked at me as he talked to me, like he hated me." Like a lamb to the slaughter. He'd heard Dad say that ages ago. Tonight he finally got it.

✦ 羊入狼群

八月对"像羊被牵入屠宰场"这句话的理解——这是全书最震撼的自省之一。一个十岁的男孩在噩梦中反复看到那些注视他的眼睛,但他在黑暗中找到了四双保护他的手。惊恐的夜晚和普通的夜晚同时发生——宇宙不在乎你的日历上标着什么颜色。
