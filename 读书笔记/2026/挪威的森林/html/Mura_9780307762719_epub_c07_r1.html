1
绿子的电话
Midori's Call · Curry, Coffee, Spaced-out
对话 日常

IN P.E. CLASS THE NEXT MORNING, THURSDAY, I SWAM SEVERAL lengths of the fifty-meter pool. The hard exercise cleared my head somewhat and gave me an appetite.

第二天早上——星期四——的体育课上，我在五十米泳池里游了好几个来回。高强度运动让头脑清醒了一些，也唤起了食欲。

"Where've you been all this time?" Midori asked. "I don't know how many times I called."

"这段时间你去哪儿了？"绿子问。"我给你打过多少次电话了。"

"Hey, what's wrong with you?" she asked. "You've got this spaced-out look. Your eyes aren't focused."

"喂，你怎么了？"她问。"一脸恍惚，眼睛都发直。"

"O.K. How about going into the city with me after that for some drinks?"

"那好。考完之后跟我去市里喝几杯怎么样？"

"For a change, why not? You look so spaced. C'mon, go drinking with me and get a little life into you. That's what I want to do—drink with you and get some life into myself. Whaddya say?"

"偶尔一次，有何不可？你看上去魂不守舍的。来吧，跟我去喝酒，给你注入点活力。我也正想这么做——跟你喝酒，也给自己注入点活力。怎么样？"

✦ 活泼的闯入者

绿子以她一贯的直率方式闯入了"我"从阿美寮回来后的恍惚状态。下午两点喝酒，这是她的提议。

2
下午喝酒
Afternoon Drinking · DUG, Vodka Tonic
对话 酒吧

AFTER GERMAN we caught a bus to Shinjuku and went to an underground bar called DUG behind the Kinokuniya bookstore. We each started with two vodka and tonics.

德语课后，我们坐公交去了新宿，来到纪伊国屋书店后面一家名叫 DUG 的地下酒吧。每人先来了两杯伏特加汤力。

"I come here once in a while. They don't embarrass you about drinking in the afternoon."

"我偶尔来这里。这里不会让你因为下午喝酒而难堪。"

"Sometimes, when the world gets hard to live in, I come here for a vodka and tonic."

"有时候，当这个世界变得难以生存，我就来这里喝伏特加汤力。"

"I've got my own special little problems."

"我有自己微不足道的特殊烦恼。"

"Like family, like boyfriends, like irregular periods. Stuff."

"比如家庭、比如男朋友、比如月经不调之类的。"

"I probably still haven't completely adapted to the world," I said after giving it some thought. "I don't know, I feel like this isn't the real world. The people, the scene: they just don't seem real to me."

"我可能还没有完全适应这个世界，"我考虑了一下说。"我不知道，我觉得这不是真实的世界。这些人、这个场景——对我来说都不像真的。"

3
绿子的幻想
Midori's Fantasies · Pirates, Donkey Shit, Uruguay
幽默 幻想

"You really ought to go to Uruguay with me," Midori said, still leaning on the bar. "Girlfriend, family, school—just dump 'em all."

"你真应该跟我去乌拉圭，"绿子依然靠在吧台上说。"女朋友、家庭、学校——全都扔了。"

"Don't you think it would be wonderful to get rid of everything and everybody and just go someplace where you don't know a soul? Sometimes I feel like doing that. I really really want to do it sometimes. So, like, say you whisked me away somewhere far far away. I'd make a pile of babies for you as tough as little bulls. And we'd all live happily ever after, rolling on the floor."

"你不觉得把一切和所有人都丢掉、去一个一个人都不认识的地方很美妙吗？有时候我就想这样。有时候真的真的很想这样。比如说，你把我带到很远很远的地方。我给你生一堆像小公牛一样结实的娃娃。然后我们永远幸福地生活在一起——在地上打滚。"

"Here I am, drinking in the afternoon, saying whatever pops into my head: 'I wanna dump everything 'n' run off somewhere.' What's the point of going to Uruguay? All they've got there is donkey shit."

"瞧我，大下午的喝酒，想到什么说什么：'想丢掉一切跑到什么地方去。'去乌拉圭有什么意义？那里只有驴粪。"

"Donkey shit everywhere. Here a shit, there a shit, the whole world is donkey shit."

"到处都是驴粪。这儿一坨，那儿一坨，整个世界都是驴粪。"

✦ 绿子的语言

直率的、有趣的、天马行空的——绿子的说话方式彻底不同于直子的含蓄与忧郁。她是《挪威的森林》中少有的喜剧性存在。

4
周日早晨
Sunday Morning · Short Skirt, Men's Dorm
日常 喜剧

MIDORI CAME TO PICK ME UP at nine-thirty on Sunday morning. I had just awakened and hadn't washed my face yet. Somebody pounded on my door and yelled, "Hey, Watanabe, it's a woman!"

星期天早上九点半，绿子来接我。我刚醒，脸还没洗。有人砰砰敲我的门喊道："喂，渡边，是个女人！"

I went down to the lobby to find Midori wearing an incredibly short jeans skirt and sitting there with her legs crossed, yawning. Every guy passing through on his way to breakfast slowed down to stare at her long, slim legs.

我下楼到大厅，发现绿子穿着一条短得惊人的牛仔裙，翘着腿坐在那儿打哈欠。每个路过去吃早饭的男生都放慢脚步，盯着她修长的腿看。

"Oh, well, it's O.K. I'm wearing really cute panties today—all pink and frilly and lacy."

"哦，没事啦。我今天穿了一条超可爱的内裤——粉色的，全是蕾丝花边。"

"That just makes it worse," I said with a sigh. I went back to my room and washed and shaved as fast as I could, put on a blue button-down shirt and a gray tweed sports coat, then went back down and hurried Midori out through the dorm gate. I was in a cold sweat.

"那更糟了，"我叹着气说。我回到房间飞快地洗了脸、刮了胡子，穿上蓝色衬衫和灰色粗花呢外套，然后下楼，把绿子匆匆带出了宿舍大门。我出了一身冷汗。

5
关于性的好奇
Sexual Curiosity · Masturbation, Blow Jobs, Case Studies
直白 对话

"Tell me, Watanabe," Midori said, looking up at the dorm buildings, "do all the guys in here masturbate, rub-a-dub-dub?"

"渡边，你告诉我，"绿子抬头看着宿舍楼说，"这里的男人都手淫吗——搓来搓去的？"

I gave up trying to avoid the question. "Well, I do, at least. I don't know about anybody else."

我放弃了回避这个问题的努力。"嗯，至少我会。别人我就不知道了。"

"Have you ever thought about me when you were doing it? Tell me the truth. I won't get mad."

"你手淫的时候想过我吗？说实话。我不会生气的。"

"Well, first of all, I think of you as a friend, so I don't want to get you involved in my sexual fantasies, and second—"

"首先，我把你当朋友，所以不想把你卷入我的性幻想。其次——"

"You have good manners even when it comes to something like this," Midori said. "That's what I like about you. Still, couldn't you allow me just one brief appearance?"

"你连这种事都这么有礼貌，"绿子说。"这就是我喜欢你的地方。不过，你就不能让我在你脑子里露一次面吗？就一次。"

✦ 毫不掩饰的好奇

绿子对性有着纯粹的好奇心。她在女子学校长大，对男生的身体和想法有着解剖学般的兴趣——直率得令人咋舌。

6
去御茶水的路上
On the Way to Ochanomizu · Intuition, Subjunctive
对话 教育

WE TOOK A COMMUTER TRAIN to Ochanomizu. When we transferred at Shinjuku I bought a thin sandwich at a stand in the station to take the place of the breakfast I hadn't eaten.

我们坐通勤电车去了御茶水。在新宿换乘时，我在车站小摊买了份薄三明治，当作没吃的早餐。

"Hey, Watanabe, can you explain the difference between the English subjunctive present and the subjunctive past?"

"喂，渡边，你能解释一下英语虚拟语气现在时和过去时的区别吗？"

"Let me ask you, then, what purpose does stuff like that serve in daily life?"

"那我问你，这种东西在日常生活中有什么用？"

"None at all," I said. "It may not serve any concrete purpose, but it does give you some kind of training to help you grasp things in general more systematically."

"一点用都没有，"我说。"也许没有具体的用途，但它能训练你从整体上更系统地把握事物。"

"You're amazing," she said. "That never occurred to me before. I always thought of things like the subjunctive case and differential calculus and chemical symbols as totally useless. A pain in the neck. So I've always ignored them. Now I have to wonder if my whole life has been a mistake."

"你太厉害了，"她说。"我以前从没这么想过。我一直觉得虚拟语气、微积分、化学符号这些东西完全没用。烦死了。所以我都置之不理。现在我开始怀疑我的人生是不是全错了。"

7
民谣俱乐部的伪善者
The Folk Club Phonies · Marx, Revolution, Rice Balls
讽刺 社会

"You know, when I entered the university, I joined a folk music club. I just wanted to sing songs. But the members were a pack of phonies."

"你知道吗，我进大学时加入了一个民谣俱乐部。我只是想唱歌。但那帮人全都是伪君子。"

"The first thing they tell you when you enter the club is you have to read Marx. 'Prepare page so-and-so to such-and-such for next time.' Somebody lectured on how folk songs have to be deeply involved with society and the radical movement."

"进俱乐部的第一件事就是让你读马克思。'回去预习第几页到第几页，下次讨论。'有人还讲座说民谣必须与社会和激进运动深刻关联。"

"But nobody was willing to explain anything to me. Far from it—they got mad at me. Can you believe it?"

"但是没有人愿意给我解释。不但不解释，他们还生我的气。你信吗？"

"One guy yelled at me, 'You stupid bitch, how do you live like that with nothing in your brain?' Well, that did it as far as I was concerned."

"有个人冲我吼：'你这个蠢货，脑袋空空你怎么活的？'好吧，我受够了。"

"After the meeting, they told me, 'Don't be silly! So what if you don't understand? Just agree with everything they say.'"

"会后他们对我说：'别傻了！不懂又怎样？顺着他们说就行了。'"

✦ 反抗伪善

绿子的独立精神在这里展露无遗。她拒绝假装理解，拒绝盲从。她的批判是全书对六十年代末学生运动最辛辣的揶揄。

8
税务官的故事
The Tax Man Story · Father, Working Class, Peace
社会 讽刺

"Believe me, I know what I'm talking about. I'm working class. Revolution or no, the working class is going to have to keep scraping by in the same old shit holes."

"相信我，我知道自己在说什么。我是工人阶级。不管有没有革命，工人阶级都不得不在同样的臭水沟里挣扎求生。"

"Tell me, Watanabe, have you ever seen a tax man?"

"告诉我，渡边，你见过税务官吗？"

"Well I have. Lots of times. They come barging in and acting big.… Meanwhile, we're crouching in the corner, and when suppertime comes we've gotta treat them to sushi deluxe—home delivered."

"我可见过。很多次。他们闯进来，趾高气昂……而我们缩在角落里。到了晚饭时间还得请他们吃豪华外卖寿司。"

"Let me tell you, though, my father never once cheated on his taxes. That's just how he is, a real old-fashioned straight arrow. But tell that to the tax man."

"不过告诉你，我父亲从来没有偷过税。他就是那么个人，老派的直性子。但你去跟税务官说这些。"

"That does it for me, then. I'm not going to believe in any damned revolution. Love is all I'm going to believe in."

"那就这样吧。我不相信什么鬼革命了。我只相信爱。"

"Peace," I said. "Peace," said Midori.

"和平。""和平。"

✦ 阶级之眼

绿子是全书最具阶级意识的人物。她的父亲经营一家小书店，一生诚实却屡遭欺压。这塑造了她对世界的不信任感。

9
医院探望
Hospital Visit · Brain Tumor, Dying Father
死亡 现实

THE UNIVERSITY HOSPITAL CORRIDORS WERE NOISY and crowded with weekend visitors and patients who had less serious symptoms, and everywhere hung that special hospital smell.

大学医院的走廊里嘈杂而拥挤，挤满了周末探病的访客和症状较轻的病人，四处弥漫着医院特有的气味。

Midori's father was in a semiprivate room, in the bed nearer the door. Stretched out, he looked like some tiny creature with a fatal wound.

绿子的父亲在半私人的病房里，靠门的床位。他摊在那儿，像一只受了致命伤的小动物。

He was going to die soon, you knew when you saw those eyes. There was no sign of life in his flesh, just the barest traces of what had once been a life.

他快要死了——看到那双眼睛你就知道。他的肉身上已没有生命的迹象，只有曾经存在过的生命的微薄痕迹。

"How are you feeling, Daddy?" said Midori, speaking into her father's ear as if testing a microphone.

"爸爸，你觉得怎么样？"绿子对着父亲的耳朵说话，好像在测试麦克风一样。

✦ 直面死亡

绿子的父亲患了和她母亲一样的脑肿瘤。绿子以平常心面对死亡——这是她疯狂幽默背后的沉重底色。

10
看护父亲
Nursing Her Father · Cucumber, Ticket, Ueno
温情 日常

Midori suddenly asked me to watch her father while she took a walk.

绿子突然让我在她出去散步时照看她父亲。

"I'll take care of your father for a while."

"我来照顾你父亲一会儿。"

After Midori left, I thought about trying to speak to her father, but I had no idea what to say to him.

绿子走后，我想试着和她父亲说说话，但不知该说什么。

How strange it would be, I thought, if this man were to breathe his last with me by his side. After all, I had just met him for the first time in my life.

我想，如果这个男人在我身边咽下最后一口气，那该有多奇怪。毕竟，我这辈子第一次见到他。

"I'm going to eat some cucumbers if you don't mind," I said to Midori's father. He didn't answer. I washed three cucumbers in the sink and dribbled a little soy sauce into a dish.

"你不介意的话，我吃点黄瓜，"我对绿子的父亲说。他没有回答。我在水槽里洗了三根黄瓜，往碟子里倒了点酱油。

"Great," I said with a smile. "With nori?"

"太好了，"我笑着说。"配紫菜？"

He ended up eating the entire cucumber. "It's good when food tastes good," I said. "It's kind of like proof you're alive."

他最终吃完了一整根黄瓜。"东西好吃真好，"我说。"这有点像活着的证明。"

✦ 临终的托付

绿子的父亲含糊地说了"车票"、"上野"、"绿子"和"拜托了"。"我"答应照看她——这个承诺贯穿了后续的故事。

11
等待与沉思
Waiting and Reflection · Sunday Letters, Spring Winding
内心 孤独

I DIDN'T GO TO THE HOSPITAL that next Sunday, though. Midori's father died on Friday morning.

但是下一个星期天我没有去医院。绿子的父亲在星期五早上去世了。

"My father died a few minutes ago," Midori said in a small, quiet voice. I asked her if there was anything I could do.

"我父亲几分钟前去世了，"绿子用细小而平静的声音说。我问她我能做什么。

"Thanks," she said. "There's really nothing. We're used to funerals. I just wanted to let you know."

"谢谢，"她说。"真的没什么。我们对葬礼已经很习惯了。只是想告诉你一声。"

A WEEK WENT BY, though, without a word from Midori. No calls, no sign of her in the classroom.

但是一个星期过去了，绿子没有任何消息。没有电话，教室里也没有她的身影。

"I think of you and Reiko and the birdhouse while I lie in bed after waking up in the morning. I think about the peacock and pigeons and parrots and turkeys—and about the rabbits. I remember the yellow rain capes that you and Reiko wore with the hoods up that rainy morning. It feels good to think about you when I'm warm in bed. I feel as if you're curled up there beside me, fast asleep. And I think how great it would be if it were true."

"早上醒来躺在床上时，我会想起你、玲子和鸟舍。想起孔雀、鸽子、鹦鹉和火鸡——还有兔子。我记得那个雨天的早晨，你和玲子穿着黄色雨披、戴着兜帽的样子。暖和地躺在被窝里想起你，感觉真好。仿佛你就蜷缩在我身边，酣然入睡。我想，如果这是真的该有多好。"

"Today's Sunday, though, a day I don't wind my spring. I've done my laundry, and now I'm in my room, writing to you."

"不过今天是星期天，我不上发条的日子。我洗了衣服，现在在房间里给你写信。"

✦ 不上发条的星期天

"我"将日常生活比作上发条——每天早晨拧三十六转，告诉自己'今天也要好好过'。只有星期天是停摆的、孤独的、用来写信的日子。
