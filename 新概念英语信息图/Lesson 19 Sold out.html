02原文故事

The play may begin at any moment I said.　It may have begun already, Susan answered.

I hurried to the ticket office. May I have two tickets please? I asked.

I'm sorry, we've sold out, the girl said.

What a pity! Susan exclaimed.

Just then, a man hurried to the ticket office.

Can I return these two tickets? he asked.　Certainly the girl said.

I went back to the ticket office at once.　Could I have those two tickets please? I asked.

Certainly the girl said, but they're for next Wednesday's performance.

Do you still want them?　　I might as well have them I said sadly.
