02原文故事

D is going to swim across the English Channel tomorrow.

She is going to set out from the French coast at five o'clock in the morning.

D is only eleven years old and she hopes to set up a new world record.

She is a strong swimmer and many people feel that she is sure to succeed.

D's father will set out with her in a small boat.

Mr. H has trained his daughter for years.

Tomorrow he will be watching her anxiously as she swims the long distance to England.

D intends to take short rests every two hours.

She will have something to drink but she will not eat any solid food.

Most of D's school friends will be waiting for her on the English coast.

Among them will be D's mother, who swam the Channel herself when she was a girl.

黛比・哈特准备明天横渡英吉利海峡。

她打算早上5点钟从法国海岸出发。

她是一个游泳能手，很多人认为她一定能成功。

黛比的父亲将乘一条小船同她一道出发。

哈特先生训练她的女儿已经多年了，

明天他将焦急地注视着女儿游过这段漫长的距离到达英国。
